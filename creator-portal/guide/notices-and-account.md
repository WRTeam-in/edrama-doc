## Notices

**Notices** collects review decisions, your submissions, release-time changes by the team, AI Studio budget alerts and other news. The **bell** in the top bar shows how many are unread.

- **Open** a notice to go to what it's about.
- **Mark as read**, or **Mark all as read**.

Most notices also arrive by email.

## Your account

Open your name at the foot of the sidebar › **Your account**.

| Card | What's there |
|---|---|
| **Profile** | What viewers see on your page in the app: **Photo** (JPG, PNG or WebP up to 2 MB) and **Bio** (with optional translations). Choose **Edit profile** to change them. Your **Verified** tick is set by the team. |
| **Sign-in** | How you sign in (Google, email or phone), when you signed in and when the session ends. |
| **Preferences** | **Time zone**: release times are shown and picked in it. **Portal language**: change it from the language menu in the top bar. These are for the portal only; your app keeps its own settings. |
| **Agreement** | **Your share** of what your series earn (coins, Series Passes, ads and VIP), the **Creator Terms** version you accepted (with **Read them**), and when your **signed agreement** was filed. The team changes these; ask them if something looks wrong. |
| **AI Studio** | Who pays for your AI calls, your Gemini key, what you used today and this month, and your monthly budget ([AI Studio](/creator-portal/guide/ai-studio/)). |

## Your page in the app

Viewers can open your creator page from any of your series ("Created by"). It shows your photo, bio, verified tick, followers and series, with a **Follow** button. Followers are told when you publish a new series. (The platform can switch creator pages off.)
