**Earnings** shows your share of what viewers paid for your series, **after store fees and refunds**. Bonus coins and your own viewing don't count. The platform pays you under your agreement.

## Where your money comes from

| Source | How you earn |
|---|---|
| **Coins** | When viewers unlock your episodes or buy your Series Pass with **paid** coins, you get your share of what those coins are worth. |
| **Ads** | Your share of the region's ad money, in proportion to the verified ad unlocks on your episodes. |
| **VIP** | Your share of the region's VIP money, in proportion to the minutes VIP members watched your locked episodes. |
| **Adjustments** | Corrections by the team (you see the amount). |

Your **share** percent is on **Your account › Agreement**. A change to it applies to new unlocks only. YouTube episodes are free and earn nothing.

## Statements

A month's statement appears **after the month ends**. Coins come first; the **Ads & VIP** part is reviewed by the team and shows *In review* until they approve it. Each statement line can show the series, the region and the paid coins, and for ads and VIP the verified ad unlocks or minutes.

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **In review** | Ads and VIP for this month are being reviewed. |
| **Approved** | Final, ready to pay. |
| **Waiting for payment** / **Partly paid** / **Paid** | Where the payment stands. |
| **Nothing to pay** | No earnings that month. |

## Payouts

The team pays you outside the portal (bank transfer, UPI, PayPal, Wise or other) and records each payment with its date, amount, method and reference, so you can match it to your bank statement.

If a later refund takes back more than a month earned, the balance comes off your next statements.
