## Ratings

The rating is **weighted** the way the app ranks series, so a handful of ratings doesn't swing it. Viewers can rate once they've reached **episode 3**. Choose a star row in the breakdown to see those reviews.

## Reply

<Tabs>
<TabItem value="reviews" label="Reviews">

**One public reply per review**, shown under the review in the app, with your name. Thank them, answer a question or share what's coming next.

If the **team** has already replied to a review, their reply stays and you can't add one.

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="comments" label="Comments">

Your reply joins the comment thread in the app, with your name and a **Creator** badge. **The viewer gets a notification.**

</TabItem>
</Tabs>

**Waiting for your reply** counts the reviews and comment threads you haven't answered. When it's empty: *You're all caught up.*

You can edit or remove your own replies at any time. To report or remove a viewer's post that breaks the rules, contact the platform's team: moderation is done in the Admin Panel.
