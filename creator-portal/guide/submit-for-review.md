Nothing you make reaches viewers until the platform's review team approves it. Every decision comes with a reason and is kept in your series' History.

## Send a new series

<Steps>

1. Open the series (a **Draft** or one with **Changes requested**) and choose **Submit for review**.
2. Check the list of episodes that go with it: only episodes that are **ready** are sent.
3. Tick the **rights declaration**: *I own this work or have the rights to publish it, including its music, images and text.*
4. Choose **Send for review**.

</Steps>

The series moves to **In review** and can't be changed meanwhile. You get a notice and an email confirming it arrived.

## Send new episodes of a live series

On a **live** series, choose **Submit new episodes**, pick the episodes, tick the rights declaration, and send. Each episode goes to the review team **on its own** and is released once approved. The series stays live as it is.

## What the reviewers check

The episodes themselves (they watch them), the subtitles, the runtime, the artwork, the synopsis and categories, your proposed free episodes and price, where it's shown, and your rights declaration. Their aim is a decision within 24 hours.

## Decisions

| Decision | What it means for you |
|---|---|
| **Approved** | A series: it goes live at its release time, or right away if none is set. The reviewer confirms (or adjusts) your free episodes, price and regions. An episode: it's released at its time. |
| **Changes requested** | Read the reason in the series' **History** (and in the email), fix it, and send again. Earlier decisions stay on record. |
| **Rejected** | It won't go live. The reason is in History. |
| **Unpublished** | A live series or episode was taken down, with the reason. Viewers who unlocked it keep it. Contact the team. |

Every decision arrives as a **notice** in the portal (the bell) and an **email**.

If viewers report a problem with one of your live episodes, the team may **ask you to fix it**: you get the reason by notice and email; the episode stays live meanwhile.

## Proposing changes to a live series

Editing a live series sends your changes as a **series update** ([Create & edit](/creator-portal/guide/create-series/#editing-a-series)). The reviewer sees each changed field side by side. Approving applies them; otherwise the series stays as it is.
