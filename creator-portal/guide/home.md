## Needs your attention

Each series where the next step is yours, with what to do:

| Status | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Draft** | Finish it and send it for review. |
| **Changes requested** | See the reason and resubmit. |
| **Not approved** | See why in its history. |

When nothing is waiting: *All caught up. Nothing is waiting on you.*

## Also on Home

- **Ratings & reviews**: your average rating across all series, and how many reviews and comments wait for your reply.
- **Your series**: your newest work, most recently changed first, with **See all**.

If you have no series yet, Home suggests starting your first one: add its details, then upload the episodes.

:::note
Figures leave out your own viewing, unlocks and follows. They're recounted every 15 minutes.
:::
