# Publishing a series

This is the creator's side. Share this page with your creators.

<Steps>
<Step title="Accept the Creator terms">

At the first sign-in, the portal shows the **Creator Terms**. Tick **I have read and agree** and **Accept and continue**. When the terms change, the portal asks again.

</Step>
<Step title="Create the series">

**New series**: the title, synopsis, categories and artwork. The series starts as a **Draft**.

</Step>
<Step title="Set the price">

The coin price per episode, within the platform's limits (30 to 90 coins and at least 5 free episodes by default). The first episodes are always free, so viewers can start the story.

</Step>
<Step title="Upload episodes and subtitles">

**Upload episodes**: drop the video files (MP4, MOV or MKV) straight from the browser. Each one shows its progress as it uploads and processes. Add **Subtitles** for each episode.

If the platform allows YouTube episodes, **Add YouTube video** adds one by its link. YouTube episodes are always free.

</Step>
<Step title="Set release times">

**Release times**: when each episode goes live. The earliest is a number of hours after the review team approves it, set by the platform.

</Step>
<Step title="Send it for review">

Confirm that you have the rights to publish it, and submit. The series shows **In review**.

</Step>
</Steps>

## After review

The review team can approve, **request changes** (with the reason), or reject. Each decision shows in **Notices** and arrives by email, and **History** lists every status change with the reviewer's reason.

- **Changes requested**: see the reason, fix it, and resubmit.
- **Live**: approved episodes go out at their release time, or at once if they have none.

## Stats

Each series has a **Stats** page. For each episode over the last days, it shows views, the share of viewers who watched to 90%, unlocks by method, and how many went on to the next episode.

The portal shows **no money figures**. Creator earnings are worked out in the monthly creator report and paid by the platform ([How earnings work](/features/how-earnings-work/)).
