# Changelog

## Version 1.0.0

The first release of eDrama.

### Viewer app (Flutter, Android and iOS)

- **[Initial release]** Guest mode with a welcome bonus; sign-in with Google, Apple, phone and email, with guest history carried over. [Accounts](/features/viewer-app/accounts/)
- **[Initial release]** Vertical player with adaptive streaming: swipe between episodes, double-tap like, hold for 2×, speed, quality (Auto to 1080p for VIP), subtitles, Up Next and clean view. [Player](/features/viewer-app/player/)
- **[Initial release]** Unlocks with coins, one ad, Series Pass or VIP, with auto-unlock and the daily spend cap. [Unlocking episodes](/features/viewer-app/unlocking-episodes/)
- **[Initial release]** Wallet with an append-only coin history, coin store, and VIP with renewal reminders. [Coins and wallet](/features/viewer-app/coins-and-wallet/)
- **[Initial release]** A Rewards tab: daily spin, streaks, shields, titles and ads for coins. [Rewards](/features/viewer-app/rewards/)
- **[Initial release]** Home built from the server, library, search, browse by category, creator profiles, notifications inbox, comments and reviews.
- **[Initial release]** Languages from the server (right-to-left supported), light and dark themes, offline cache and screen protection.

### Admin Panel

- **[Initial release]** Dashboard, titles and title editor, categories, uploads (resumable, CSV, URL and YouTube), release schedule, Home builder and website editor. [Using the Admin Panel](/admin-panel/using/)
- **[Initial release]** Creators and review queue, comments and reviews, AI usage.
- **[Initial release]** Users, balance adjustments (with a second approver), support tickets and push.
- **[Initial release]** Monetization per region (regions, coin packs, VIP plans, ads, rewards and the fair-play rules), and series shown in some regions only.
- **[Initial release]** Transactions with refunds and exports, the monthly creator report, Creator revenue, creator statements and recorded payouts.
- **[Initial release]** Settings, team and roles with 18 permission areas, audit log, System, License & updates and Clear demo data.
- **[Initial release]** Files on this server, Amazon S3 (or an S3-compatible service) or Cloudflare R2; video on this server with ffmpeg, or on Cloudflare Stream.
- **[Initial release]** AI Studio with Google Gemini: characters, storyboards, video takes and the episode editor.

### Creator Portal

- **[Initial release]** A portal for approved creators: apply or be invited, Creator terms, series, uploads, review of every episode, release times, stats, comments and reviews, their own earnings, AI Studio and notices. [Creator Portal](/creator-portal/)

### Website

- **[Initial release]** Public website, edited from the Admin Panel, in several languages. [Website](/website/)

### Installation

- **[Initial release]** Four-step web installer with demo data, licence activation and one-version-at-a-time update packages. [Installation](/admin-panel/installation/)
