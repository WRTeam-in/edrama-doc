# Changelog

## Version 1.0.0

- **[Initial release]** Flutter viewer app for Android and iOS: guest mode, vertical player with adaptive streaming, unlocks with coins, one ad, Series Pass or VIP, wallet, coin store, a Rewards tab (daily spin, streaks and ads for coins), library, search, creator profiles and notifications
- **[Initial release]** Admin Panel: dashboard, titles, categories, uploads, release schedule, Home builder, website editor, creators and review queue, comments and reviews, users, balance adjustments, support tickets, push, monetization per region, transactions, settings, team and roles, audit log
- **[Initial release]** Prices, coin packs, VIP plans and ads per region, and series shown in some regions only
- **[Initial release]** Monthly creator report, creator statements and recorded payouts
- **[Initial release]** Creator Portal for approved creators, with review of every episode and their own earnings
- **[Initial release]** Public website, edited from the Admin Panel, in several languages
- **[Initial release]** Video on this server with ffmpeg, on Amazon S3, or on Cloudflare Stream
- **[Initial release]** Web installer, licence activation and update packages
