## Before you write to us

Including these helps us answer in one go:

- Your **CodeCanyon purchase code** ([where to find it](https://help.market.envato.com/hc/en-us/articles/202822600-Where-Is-My-Purchase-Code-)) and your **domain**.
- What you did, what you expected, and what happened, with the **exact error text** or a screenshot.
- For the server: your **PHP version** (`php -v`), the last lines of `storage/logs/laravel.log`, and what <AdminPath path="System › System" /> shows for the queues and scheduled tasks.
- For the app: the app version (bottom of the **Me** tab), the phone model and OS version.
- For app builds: the output of `flutter --version` and the **full** build error.

Many answers are already in the [FAQs](/faqs/) and [Troubleshooting](/troubleshooting/).

:::warning Never send passwords or keys by email
We'll never ask for your database password, `APP_KEY` or service-account files. If we need access to your server, we'll agree a secure way first.
:::

## What support covers

- Installation and configuration help
- Bugs in eDrama, and their fixes
- Guidance on features and settings
- Help with updates

Setting up your own computer for Flutter (Android Studio, Xcode), your hosting account, store developer accounts, and custom changes to the code aren't included. Ask us about a quote for those.

## Installation service

Don't want to set it up yourself? We can install and configure eDrama for you: install the server, set up cron, queues and ffmpeg, connect Firebase, the stores and AdMob, and build and publish your branded app. Email us with your purchase code.
