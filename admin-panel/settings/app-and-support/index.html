App and support

Where: Admin Panel›System›Settings›App & support

Everything here reaches the app at its next start, without a new release.

App versions​

For Android and iOS:

FieldWhat it does
LatestYour newest version in the store.
Minimum supportedBuilds below it must update before they can be used: the app shows its update screen. Can't be newer than Latest.
Store linkYour app's Google Play or App Store page. The app's update screen and the website's store buttons use it.

Support email​

Where viewers and creators write when they need help. It shows in the app's help screen, in creator emails and to suspended creators. Leave it blank to use EDRAMA_SUPPORT_EMAIL from .env, which is support@example.com until you change it, so set one of the two.

Comment blocklist​

A comment or review containing one of these words or phrases waits for a person on Admin Panel›Community›Comments & reviews. Whole words, any case. Press Enter after each.

Features​

Switch a feature off and the app hides it at its next start.

SwitchWhat it controls
VIPSubscriptions that unlock every drama
Series PassOne price for a whole series
Auto-unlockUnlocks the next episode with coins as it starts
Rewards (daily spin and ads for coins)The daily spin, streak titles and Rewards ads
Phone sign-inPhone number sign-in, in the app and on the Creator Portal
Episode commentsComments under episodes
Series reviewsRatings and reviews on series pages

Maintenance mode​

Turn on Show the maintenance screen and write a Message (up to 280 characters, such as when you'll be back). While it's on, the app shows the maintenance screen with your message instead of the catalogue.
