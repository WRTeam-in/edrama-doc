Branding and sign-in screen

Branding​

Where: Admin Panel›System›Settings›Branding

One place sets your brand for the app, the Admin Panel, the Creator Portal and the website. A preview of the app and the Admin Panel sits beside the form.

FieldWhat it is
App name, TaglineYour product's name and one-line tagline. The app shows the name once it reaches the server.
Horizontal logo for dark backgroundsA logo with a light name. It replaces the mark and name in the sidebar and on the share and error pages.
Horizontal logo for light backgroundsA logo with a dark name, for the top bar and public pages in the light theme.
Vertical logoThe mark above the name, with a light name, for the dark sign-in screens.
FaviconThe small icon on the browser tab. Square, at least 64 × 64 pixels.
Square logoThe mark alone, for small spots such as the push preview.
Wordmark fontInstrument Serif Italic or Geist Bold, for the name where it's written out.
Theme colourOne colour for the app, the Admin Panel and the Creator Portal: buttons, switches, progress and highlights. Pick Greenlight (default), Ember, Iris, Rosé, Aqua, Gold, Sky, or a Custom colour by hex code. Text on it switches between ink and white so it stays readable.
Studio opens inThe theme the Admin Panel and Creator Portal open in: Paper (light), Ink (dark) or Follow the device. Each member can still switch with the moon icon.

Logos are PNG or WebP, up to 1 MB, on a transparent background.

The app's own logo

The logo inside the app (splash screen, Home header) ships with the app. Change it in the app's code: see Mobile App › Logo and icons. The app picks up the App name and Theme colour from here at its next start.

Sign-in screen​

Admin Panel›System›Settings›Sign-in screen sets the stage beside the Admin Panel's and the Creator Portal's sign-in forms.

  • Photos: the built-in photos, or up to 3 of your own landscape photos (JPEG, PNG or WebP up to 4 MB each; 1600 × 1000 or larger looks best). Drag to set their order.
  • Motion: Cinematic (photos cross-fade and drift, posters scroll, the tagline rises word by word), Subtle (only the photos cross-fade) or Still. Anyone whose device asks for less motion always gets a still stage.
  • Show the live titles' posters: a strip of your newest live series beside the photos.
  • Words: the tagline and the line under it, separately for the Admin Panel's and the Creator Portal's sign-in.
