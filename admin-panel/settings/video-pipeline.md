# Video pipeline

**Where:** <AdminPath path="System › Settings › Video pipeline" />

Where videos are stored and processed is set on [Storage & video](./storage-and-video.md).

## Uploads

Episode files go from the browser straight to where videos live, as MP4, MOV or MKV.

- **Largest file**: larger files are refused before they upload. 20 GB to start, up to 200 GB.
- **Allow YouTube videos as episodes**: adds **Add YouTube video** for your team and creators. These episodes are **always free**, because YouTube doesn't allow charging for them. Turning this off keeps those already added.

## Creator uploads

Limits for creators in the [Creator Portal](/creator-portal/):

| Field | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Largest file a creator uploads** | 2 GB to start, up to the team's limit above |
| **Episodes a creator has processing at once** | 10 to start. More wait until one finishes. |
| **Earliest release after approval** | A creator's release time must be at least this many hours after your team approves the episode. `0` allows any time from now. 2 hours to start. |
