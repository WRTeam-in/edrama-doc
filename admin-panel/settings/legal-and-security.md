# Legal pages and security

## Legal pages

**Where:** <AdminPath path="System › Settings › Legal pages" />

Seven pages, shown in the app and on the website:

- Terms of service
- Privacy policy
- Creator terms (creators accept these before they upload)
- Content licences & credits
- Refund policy
- Community guidelines
- Takedown requests (DMCA)

**Edit** a page, write its **Title** and **Text** in Markdown (headings, lists and links show in the app), and **Publish**. Each publish adds a **new version** and keeps the earlier ones, so what a viewer agreed to can be shown later. Each page is public at `/legal/<page>` on your Admin Panel's domain.

**Search & sharing** on each page sets an optional search title and description per language, or hides the page from search engines.

:::caution Write your own legal text
Store reviewers check that your privacy policy and terms describe your app. Have them written for your business and country.
:::

**Where:** <AdminPath path="System › Settings › Sign-in & security" />
