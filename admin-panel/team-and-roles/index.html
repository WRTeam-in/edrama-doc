Team and roles

Your team signs in to the Admin Panel. Each member has one role, and each role allows None, View or Edit in each area. Manage both in Admin Panel›System›Team & roles.

Add a member​

You have two ways:

  • Invite: enter their email and role. They get an email link to set their own password. The link works once and for 7 days. Needs email delivery.
  • Add member: set a Temporary password (at least 12 characters) and share it with them securely. They must choose their own password at their first sign-in.

You can later Change role, Disable (they are signed out on their next click) and Enable a member. Every change is recorded in the audit log.

The roles​

The Owner can do everything and is the only one who manages the team and edits roles. Four ready-made roles come with eDrama, and you can create your own with New role.

RoleWhat it can do
AdminEverything except team access; the audit log is view only. Refunds up to 100.00 in your reporting currency.
Content ManagerTitles, uploads and the release schedule; can look at the review queue.
ModeratorThe review queue and creators.
Finance & SupportUsers, balance adjustments and refunds; can look at transactions and prices. Refunds up to 20.00. Give it Transactions › Edit if it should receive the monthly creator report.

The areas a role can be given are: Titles & episodes, Uploads, Schedule & Home, Review queue, Creators, Users, Balance adjustments, Refunds, Transactions, Prices & coin packs, Push, Settings, Team & roles, and Audit log.

Two-factor authentication​

Each member can turn on two-factor sign-in in Your account, with an authenticator app (Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator, 1Password and the like). They get 8 recovery codes.

To make it required for everyone, the Owner turns it on in Admin Panel›System›Settings›Sign-in & security. Team & roles shows who has it on.

Audit log​

Admin Panel›System›Audit log records every action the team takes: who did what, and when. Rows can't be edited or deleted.
