Admin Panel menus

Every page of the Admin Panel, in the order of the sidebar. Team members only see the pages their role allows.

Overview​

Dashboard​

How the platform is doing today: viewers, unlocks, revenue and more, in your reporting currency. Figures refresh every 15 minutes.

Content​

Titles​

Every drama, its episodes, its prices per region and who owns it. A title is one of three kinds:

  • Your own series (an Original),
  • a Licensed series, with the licensor's share of net revenue from your contract,
  • a Creator series, made by an invited creator who earns their share of every paid unlock.

Every title starts as a draft: add episodes, artwork and prices, send it to review, then publish. Prices set the coin price per episode and the Series Pass per region; a region without its own price uses the default region's. Price lock keeps the price each viewer started with. Episodes have subtitles, and a series can be marked Complete once every episode is out.

Categories​

The labels on your titles, and the picks in the app's onboarding. Drag to set the order the app shows them in.

Upload & pipeline​

Upload episode files (MP4, MOV or MKV), many at once. Each goes Uploading → Processing → Ready, turned into vertical streaming video in the background. See Storage and video.

Release schedule​

Episodes go out at each one's publish time. Drag an episode to move it, and pick the time zone to read the times in.

Home builder​

Build the app's Home from sections: add, set up, drag to reorder, and choose who sees each one. A phone preview updates as you edit.

Website​

Your public website's sections, header, footer, look and search settings. Edit, preview, then publish. See Website › Edit the website.

Community​

Creators​

Invited creators, their revenue share, and new applications. See Becoming a creator.

Review queue​

Creator titles, new episodes and viewer reports waiting for a decision. Every decision is logged. See The review queue.

Comments & reviews​

What viewers say about your titles. Check reports, approve what was held by the blocklist, and remove what breaks the rules.

Audience​

Users​

Find a viewer by name, email or ID, and open their profile, wallet and coin history, unlocks, purchases and devices. Wallet changes always need a reason and are logged. From a viewer you can adjust their balance, grant an episode, or ban the account.

Balance adjustments​

Coins added or removed by the team. Over the Approval limit (Settings › Payments), a second member approves.

Support tickets​

What viewers sent from the app's Help screen. Reply by email, then record where each one stands.

Push​

Send a notification to every viewer or to a title's followers, now or at a set time. Pushes respect each viewer's quiet hours and daily cap.

Money​

Monetization​

Coins, VIP, ads and rewards, set per region, and the locked fair-play rules. See Monetization.

Transactions​

Every purchase across Google Play and the App Store. Receipts are verified on the server before coins land. Refunds from the store arrive by themselves; refunds from the panel are limited by each role's refund limit. Export to CSV.

System​

Settings​

Everything you configure without touching code. See Settings.

Team & roles​

Members, invites, roles and their permissions. See Team and roles.

Audit log​

Append-only: who did what, to which record, and why. It can be exported.

System​

Background jobs, queues, scheduled tasks and failed jobs, with retry. See Background jobs.

License & updates​

Your purchase code and installing updates. See License and updates.

Clear demo data​

Shown only to the Owner while demo data is imported. See First steps.
