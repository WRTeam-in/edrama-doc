# Earnings calculator

Move the sliders or type numbers to see how one month of a series splits between the stores, its creator (or licensor) and you. It uses the same formulas as eDrama's [monthly creator report](./how-earnings-work.md), with eDrama's starting values filled in.

<EarningsCalculator />

:::note What this assumes
Every paid coin brought in the full coin value and nothing was refunded. In the real report, discounted packs lower the **paid coin value** (never above the coin value), refunds take back what their coins earned, and Series Passes bought with bonus coins earn nothing. The ad and VIP part is the amount your team adds to the creator's statement; here it's shared at the same percent as coins.
:::

:::note What this doesn't include
Your own running costs: hosting, video storage and streaming (for example Cloudflare Stream's minutes), taxes and payment payouts. Store fees also differ by country and programme; enter the fees you actually pay.
:::
