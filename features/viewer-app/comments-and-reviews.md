## Comments on an episode

Tap **Comments** in the player. Comments belong to an episode, and each can be tied to a **moment** in it: *E7 · tap a time to jump there.*

- **Sort**: Top, By moment, Newest.
- **Write**: *Add a comment at 0:42…*. Mark it as a **Spoiler** to hide it until tapped (*Spoiler from E7 · tap to show*).
- **Reply**, **Like**, **Report**, view more replies.
- Replies from the series' creator carry a **Creator** badge; replies from your team are marked **Team**. A **Pinned** comment stays on top.

A comment with a blocked word, a link or a phone number waits for a quick check: *Waiting for a quick check before others see it.* (Only its author sees it meanwhile.)

## Ratings and reviews

On the series page, **Rate** once the viewer has reached **episode 3**: stars, and optional words. The score shown is weighted, so a few 5-star ratings can't outrank a series with thousands.

The **Reviews** page lists them with helpful votes, the reviewer's episodes watched, and the creator's or team's reply. Viewers can mark a review **helpful** or **report** it.

Reports and held posts go to your team on [Comments & reviews](/admin-panel/using/community/comments-and-reviews/). Comments and reviews can each be switched off in the Admin Panel.
