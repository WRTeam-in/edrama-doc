## The daily spin

<Steps>

1. Tap **Spin for free**. The wheel lifts above the page and spins; tap the wheel to skip ahead.
2. It lands on a prize, such as **+50**, *about 1 episode*. VIP members see *VIP ×2* and win double.
3. **Collect**: the coins fly to the balance.

</Steps>

**See the odds** shows every prize's chance. Your team sets the prizes and chances per region ([Ads & rewards](/admin-panel/monetization/#ads)).

## Streaks

Each day's spin adds a day to the streak. *Today isn't counted yet. Spin the wheel to keep your 7-day streak.* Miss a day and the streak restarts, unless a **shield** saves it.

**Streak shields**: earned every 7 days of streak (default), up to 2 held. A shield covers one missed day by itself: *Your shield saved your 12-day streak.* Shields are never sold.

**Titles**: names earned at the viewer's **best** streak and kept for good (Extra, Understudy, Scene Stealer, Plot Twister, Drama Champion, Drama Royalty, Drama Legend, Hall of Famer), each paying its bonus coins once. A new title appears with a celebration; **Titles** lists them all with the days to go. The top title shows *You've reached the very top*.

**Share** the streak as a card: *12 days in a row on eDrama 🔥 Plot Twister*.

## Earn more

**Watch an ad** pays bonus coins (15 by default) per ad, up to the daily limit, 30 seconds at most, never a playable ad. Coins arrive once AdMob confirms the ad (*Your coins are on the way*).

## Keep watching

After a reward, a **Keep watching** card offers the next locked episode the new coins can unlock.

## When Rewards is off

If your team switches Rewards off, the tab becomes **Wallet**, with the balance, coin packs and history.

:::note
Bonus coins expire (7 days by default) and are always spent before paid coins. *Spin once a day for free. Every spin pays bonus coins, which are spent first and expire soon after you earn them.*
:::
