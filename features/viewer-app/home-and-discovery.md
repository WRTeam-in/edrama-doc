## Home

Home is built by your team in the [Home builder](/admin-panel/using/content/home-builder/), so what viewers see depends on your layout and on **who** they are (guest, signed in, VIP) and **where** they are (their region).

| Section | What the viewer does |
|---|---|
| **Featured series** (hero) | Swipe through big cards; tap **Play E1** (or **Continue E7**) or **Details**. Shows *First 5 free* or *Complete · 24 eps*. |
| **Continue watching** | Pick up where they stopped: *E2 · 0:03 in*, *E3 ready to watch*, *E8 · 60 coins*, or *E9 drops soon*. |
| **Rows and grids** | Tap a poster to open the series; **See all** opens the full list. Top 10 rows show *Number 1*, *Number 2*… |
| **Banners and promo cards** | Open a series, a category, the store, VIP, Rewards or a web page. |
| **Category chips** | Open a category's page. |
| **Notes** | Short lines from your team, such as the fair-pricing promise. |

Pull down to refresh. If nothing is published yet: *Nothing to watch yet.*

- Before typing: **Recent** searches (with **Clear all**), **Browse by category** and **Trending**.
- Results cover **titles**, **categories** and **creators** (creators only while creator pages are on).
- No results: *Try a category, a title or a creator's name.*

## Browse

A category, or **See all** on a row, opens **Browse**:

- **Filter**: All, Complete, Ongoing.
- **Sort by**: Most popular, Newest, Top rated.
- The count of titles, then a grid of posters.

**Every genre** (See all on a category row) shows all categories as tiles; tapping one flies its picture into the category's page.

## Creator pages

Tap **Created by** on a series to open the creator's page: photo, **Verified creator** tick, followers, series count, bio, and their series. **Follow** to be told when they publish a new series. **Share** sends a link to their page. Creator pages can be switched off in the Admin Panel.

## Shared links

Links viewers share (`…/s/12` for a series, `…/c/name` for a creator) open straight in the app on phones that have it, and on the website's share page otherwise. See [Deep links](/mobile-app/deep-links/).
