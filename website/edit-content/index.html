Edit the website

Everything on the website is edited in Admin Panel›Content›Website. A live preview sits beside the form and shows what the form holds now, on a computer or a phone, in any language.

Who can edit it

Members with View on Schedule & Home can open this page, and Edit is needed to change it. The Website address and the refresh secret need Settings › Edit. See Team and roles.

Draft, preview, publish​

ActionWhat it does
Save draftKeeps your changes. Visitors don't see them.
PreviewOpens the saved draft on your real website for 30 minutes. Nothing is published.
PublishMakes the draft live. Visitors see it within a minute. You can write what changed.
Discard draftThrows away unpublished changes and goes back to the live version.
Switch website off / onOff shows visitors a short "back soon" page and asks search engines not to index it.

Every publish is kept in Connection & history. Load into draft brings an earlier version back so you can check it and publish it again.

The tabs​

Sections​

The home page is a list of sections you add, reorder, hide and style. Each section type has several Style choices.

SectionWhat it shows
Top sliderBig pictures with your headline and buttons. Picks your most popular series by itself, or shows slides you make.
NumbersSeries, episodes, categories and creators counted from your catalogue, or numbers you type.
Series showcaseA row or grid of series: new, popular, Top 10, complete, one category, one creator, or ones you pick.
What is it?A few lines about your app, with feature points and phone pictures.
CategoriesYour categories with their artwork, each leading to its category page.
Get the appStore buttons, a direct APK link and a QR code. Always on the page.
CreatorsCreators with live series, and an invitation to become one.
QuestionsQuestions and short answers. Search engines and AI assistants read these too.
Call to actionA bold band with a heading and a button.
Text blockA heading, a few paragraphs and an optional picture.

In headings, put *stars* around a word or two to set them in the accent style. In text, **bold**, *italic* and [links](https://…) work.

Header & footer​

The header style (Floating, Classic, Centred, Minimal), the footer style (Columns, Big name, Compact, Download first) and your Social links, shown as icons in the footer of every page.

Look & languages​

The theme (Midnight (dark), Paper (light) or Follow the visitor's device), heading style, corners and motion, and which languages the website comes in. See Languages and app links.

Translations​

Pick a language, then type each text in it. A box left empty shows the default language's text. Keep words in braces or after a colon, such as {app_name} or :count, exactly as they are: they are filled in for you.

Search & statistics​

  • Search engines and sharing: site title, description, share picture, whether search engines may index the site, whether AI crawlers may read it, llms.txt, and Google and Bing verification codes.
  • Page by page: an optional search title, description and share picture for each kind of page (home, all series, all categories, each series, each category, each creator), in each language.
  • Analytics: Plausible, Umami or Google Analytics. Google Analytics loads only after the visitor agrees.

Connection & history​

The Website address, the refresh secret and the publish history. See Connect to the Admin Panel.

Sample series

While a series section has fewer than 4 live series, sample series fill it so the page never looks empty. Turn this off when your catalogue is ready.
