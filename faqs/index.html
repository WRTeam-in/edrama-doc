Frequently asked questions

Installation​

/install shows "404"​

The installer closes itself once installation finishes. Sign in at /admin instead. If you need to install again on an empty database, contact support.

The installer says something is "Missing"​

Your server lacks a PHP extension, runs PHP below 8.5, or a folder isn't writable. Fix the items marked Missing (or ask your host), then press Check again. See Server requirements.

The Admin Panel asks for the purchase code again, or says the domain doesn't match​

Your purchase code is registered to one domain. If you moved, free it on the old domain with Reset purchase code, then activate it on the new one. See License and updates. The app and the website keep working meanwhile.

Does it work on shared hosting?​

Yes. The single cron job runs the background work, so you need no long-running process. For video, set up a ready-made ffmpeg or use Cloudflare Stream.

Background work​

Emails, pushes or video processing don't happen​

The cron job isn't running. Add it as on Cron and background jobs, then check Admin Panel›System›System. For emails, also check Email delivery (SMTP): without it, emails only go to the server's log.

An episode stays "Processing"​

  • With this server or Amazon S3: ffmpeg isn't found or the host stops long jobs. Check the ffmpeg line on Settings › Storage & video.
  • With Cloudflare Stream: the webhook isn't registered. Run php artisan edrama:video-webhook.

Videos stuck in processing are also checked every 10 minutes.

The app​

The app can't reach my server​

Check apiBaseUrl in lib/app/config/app_config.dart: it must be https:// and end in /api/v1/. If you turned on App Check with EDRAMA_APP_CHECK=enforce, test with monitor first.

The app shows "maintenance" or asks to update​

Maintenance mode is on, or the app's version is below Minimum supported. Both are in Settings › App & support.

Shared links open the browser, not the app​

Check the .well-known files and the app's link domains: Share links and Deep links. On Android, include the Play App Signing SHA-256 fingerprint.

How do I add a language?​

Upload its JSON file in Settings › Regions & languages. The app downloads it at its next start, with no new release. See Languages.

Money​

A purchase didn't add coins​

Check the Google Play or App Store keys and press Test connection in Settings › Payments, and that the product IDs in Monetization match the store. The purchase shows in Admin Panel›Money›Transactions.

Ads don't unlock episodes​

Check that the app has your own AdMob app IDs (not Google's test IDs), that the region has ad unit IDs in Monetization › Ads, and that server-side verification is on in AdMob with your callback URL. An ad pays only once AdMob confirms it.

Can I charge different prices in different countries?​

Yes. Create a region for those countries in Monetization › Regions, with its own currency, coin value, packs, VIP plans and title prices.

Why can't I raise a series' price for people already watching it?​

Price lock is a fair-play rule: once a viewer starts a series, their per-episode price can't rise. A new price applies to viewers who haven't started it, and a price drop applies to everyone.

How are creators paid?​

eDrama works out each creator's share in the monthly creator report, and your team pays them from it. See How earnings work.
