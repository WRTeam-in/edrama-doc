AdMob rewarded ads​

Create your AdMob account and add the Android and iOS apps: common guide › Google AdMob.

eDrama uses two rewarded ad units: one that unlocks an episode, and one for Rewards (bonus coins). What goes where:

WhatWhere
AdMob app ID, Androidandroid/app/src/main/AndroidManifest.xml: the com.google.android.gms.ads.APPLICATION_ID value
AdMob app ID, iOSios/Runner/Info.plist: GADApplicationIdentifier
Ad unit IDsAdmin Panel › Money › Monetization › Ads, per region. Not in the app.
Server-side verificationAdMob › each ad unit › Server-side verification: https://your-domain.com/api/v1/webhooks/admob-ssv
Replace the test IDs

The app ships with Google's sample test app IDs. Put your own in both files before you release.

An ad unlocks an episode or pays coins only once AdMob confirms it on that callback, so server-side verification is required.

Consent​

The app asks for ad consent with Google's User Messaging Platform. Create the messages in AdMob › Privacy & messaging: a GDPR message for Europe, and an IDFA message (Apple's tracking prompt) for iOS. Viewers can reopen their choices in Me › Settings › Privacy choices.

In-app purchases​

Coins are consumable products and VIP plans are auto-renewable subscriptions. The Series Pass is paid in coins, so it needs no store product.

  1. 1

    Create the products

    In Play Console and App Store Connect, create one consumable product per coin pack (common guide › In-app purchases) and one auto-renewable subscription per VIP plan (VIP subscriptions).

  2. 2

    Enter the same IDs in the Admin Panel

    Monetization › Coin packs: Google Play product ID and App Store product ID for each pack. Monetization › VIP plans: Google Play subscription ID, Google Play base plan ID and App Store product ID.

  3. 3

    Connect the stores

    Add the Google Play and App Store keys and webhooks so the server can check every purchase: Settings › Payments.

The app never decides how many coins a purchase gives: it sends the store's receipt to your server, which checks it with the store and adds the coins. Restore purchases in the coin store claims anything interrupted.

To test, use Google Play's licence testers and Apple's Sandbox accounts, and set the App Store card's Environment to Sandbox.
