Integrations

eDrama connects to a handful of outside services. Each one's keys go in a card in Admin Panel›Settings with a Where do I find these? guide, Save and Test connection.

AdMobRewarded ads; earnings for creator revenueIf you use adsMonetization › Ads; Settings › Creator revenueAdMob
Amazon S3 / S3-compatibleStoring files and videosOptionalSettings › Storage & videoStorage & streaming

Callback addresses​

Several services call your server back. Each card in Settings shows its exact address with a Copy button. They're all listed here too: type your Admin Panel's domain once and every address fills in.

https://

Every address on these pages fills in with it, ready to copy. It never leaves this browser.

ServiceAddress
Google Play real-time notifications
The Pub/Sub push subscription's endpoint and its audience (Google Play)		https://admin.your-domain.com/api/v1/webhooks/google-play
App Store Server Notifications
App Store Connect › your app › App Information, Version 2, for production and sandbox (App Store)		https://admin.your-domain.com/api/v1/webhooks/app-store
AdMob server-side verification
AdMob › each rewarded ad unit › Server-side verification (AdMob)		https://admin.your-domain.com/api/v1/webhooks/admob-ssv
Cloudflare Stream webhook
Registered for you by php artisan edrama:video-webhook (Cloudflare Stream)		https://admin.your-domain.com/api/v1/webhooks/video
AdMob OAuth redirect
Google Cloud › your OAuth client › Authorised redirect URIs, for Connect AdMob (AdMob)		https://admin.your-domain.com/admin/admob/callback
Android app links
Nothing to paste: open it to check it lists your app (Deep links)		https://admin.your-domain.com/.well-known/assetlinks.json
iOS universal links
Nothing to paste: open it to check it lists your app		https://admin.your-domain.com/.well-known/apple-app-site-association
tip

If you move to another domain, every one of these changes with it. Update each service, or notifications stop reaching your server.
