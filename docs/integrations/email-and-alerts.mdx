## SMTP email

eDrama sends team invites, password resets, every creator email (invites, review decisions, suspensions…) and system alerts. Connect your provider in <Path>Settings › Email delivery (SMTP)</Path> ([full page](../admin/settings/email.mdx)).

| Provider | Host | Port / encryption | Username |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Gmail / Google Workspace** | `smtp.gmail.com` | 587 STARTTLS | Your address; password = an [app password](https://myaccount.google.com/apppasswords) |
| **Amazon SES** | `email-smtp.<region>.amazonaws.com` | 587 STARTTLS | SES SMTP credentials |
| **SendGrid** | `smtp.sendgrid.net` | 587 STARTTLS | `apikey` (password = your API key) |
| **Any other** | Your provider's SMTP host | 587 STARTTLS or 465 SSL/TLS | Your SMTP login |

The **From address** must be one your provider lets you send from (a verified sender or domain). **Save**, then **Send test email**.

:::tip[Land in the inbox, not spam]
Set up **SPF**, **DKIM** and **DMARC** records for your sending domain, as your provider describes. It's the single biggest factor in deliverability.
:::

## System alerts

When a background job or scheduled task fails, eDrama alerts the team within minutes. In <Path>Settings › Notifications › System alerts</Path>:

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Alert emails** | One or more addresses, separated by commas. Members with Settings (Edit) also get a bell. |
| **Slack webhook URL** | Optional. [api.slack.com/apps](https://api.slack.com/apps) › Create New App › **Incoming Webhooks** › Add New Webhook to Workspace › pick the channel › copy the URL. |

**Test connection** sends a test alert. Alerts are throttled to one per job per 15 minutes, and the license check also alerts here if your purchase code is refused.
