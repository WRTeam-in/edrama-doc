eDrama connects to a handful of outside services. Each one's keys go in a card in <Path>Settings</Path> with a **Where do I find these?** guide, **Save** and **Test connection**.

| Service | Used for | Needed? | Keys go in | Guide |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Firebase** | Viewer sign-in, push, App Check, Creator Portal sign-in | Yes | Settings › Notifications | [Firebase](firebase.mdx) |
| **Google Play** | Coin packs and VIP on Android; refunds and renewals | For Android | Settings › Payments | [Google Play](google-play.mdx) |
| **App Store** | Coin packs and VIP on iOS; refunds and renewals | For iOS | Settings › Payments | [App Store](app-store.mdx) |
| **AdMob** | Rewarded ads; earnings for creator revenue | If you use ads | Monetization › Ads; Settings › Creator revenue | [AdMob](admob.mdx) |
| **Amazon S3 / S3-compatible** | Storing files and videos | Optional | Settings › Storage & video | [Storage & streaming](storage-and-streaming.mdx) |
| **Cloudflare R2** | Storing files and videos, no download fees | Optional | Settings › Storage & video | [Storage & streaming](storage-and-streaming.mdx) |
| **Cloudflare Stream** | Processing and streaming videos | Optional | Settings › Storage & video | [Storage & streaming](storage-and-streaming.mdx) |
| **ffmpeg** | Processing videos on your server | Unless you use Stream | Server | [Video processing](../installation/video-processing.mdx) |
| **SMTP email** | Invites, resets, creator emails, alerts | Strongly recommended | Settings › Email delivery | [Email & alerts](email-and-alerts.mdx) |
| **Slack** | System alerts | Optional | Settings › Notifications | [Email & alerts](email-and-alerts.mdx) |
| **Google Gemini** | AI Studio | Optional | Settings › AI Studio; creators' accounts | [Gemini & YouTube](gemini-and-youtube.mdx) |
| **YouTube** | Free episodes from YouTube | Optional | Settings › Video pipeline (switch) | [Gemini & YouTube](gemini-and-youtube.mdx) |
| **WRTeam license server** | Purchase code checks | Yes | Installer; System › License & updates | [License & updates](../installation/license-and-updates.mdx) |

## Callback addresses

Several services call your server back. Each card in Settings shows its exact address with a **Copy** button. They're all listed here too: type your Admin Panel's domain once and every address fills in.

<DomainInput />

| Service | Address |
|---|---|
| **Google Play real-time notifications**<br />The Pub/Sub push subscription's endpoint and its audience ([Google Play](google-play.mdx)) | <ServerUrl path="/api/v1/webhooks/google-play" /> |
| **App Store Server Notifications**<br />App Store Connect › your app › App Information, **Version 2**, for production and sandbox ([App Store](app-store.mdx)) | <ServerUrl path="/api/v1/webhooks/app-store" /> |
| **AdMob server-side verification**<br />AdMob › each rewarded ad unit › Server-side verification ([AdMob](admob.mdx)) | <ServerUrl path="/api/v1/webhooks/admob-ssv" /> |
| **Cloudflare Stream webhook**<br />Registered for you by `php artisan edrama:video-webhook` ([Cloudflare Stream](storage-and-streaming.mdx#cloudflare-stream)) | <ServerUrl path="/api/v1/webhooks/video" /> |
| **AdMob OAuth redirect**<br />Google Cloud › your OAuth client › Authorised redirect URIs, for **Connect AdMob** ([AdMob](admob.mdx)) | <ServerUrl path="/admin/admob/callback" /> |
| **Android app links**<br />Nothing to paste: open it to check it lists your app ([Deep links](../app-setup/deep-links.mdx)) | <ServerUrl path="/.well-known/assetlinks.json" /> |
| **iOS universal links**<br />Nothing to paste: open it to check it lists your app | <ServerUrl path="/.well-known/apple-app-site-association" /> |

:::tip
If you move to another domain, every one of these changes with it. Update each service, or notifications stop reaching your server.
:::
