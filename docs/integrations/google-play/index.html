Google Play

Your server talks to Google Play to verify every coin-pack purchase and VIP subscription, to refund from the Admin Panel, and to hear about refunds, renewals and cancellations through real-time developer notifications.

1. Service account​

  1. In the Google Cloud console, in the project linked to your Play account, enable the Google Play Android Developer API.
  2. IAM & Admin › Service accounts › Create service account, then its Keys › Add key › JSON. A file downloads.
  3. In Play Console › Users and permissions › Invite new users: invite the service account's email (it ends in iam.gserviceaccount.com), with View financial data, orders and cancellation survey responses and Manage orders and subscriptions, for your app.
note

New Play Console access can take up to a day to start working. If Test connection fails straight after, try again later.

2. Enter the keys​

Admin Panel›Settings›Payments › Google Play card:

FieldValue
Package nameYour app's ID, such as com.yourcompany.drama.
Service account JSONThe whole file.
Pub/Sub push service accountThe account the push subscription signs with (step 3 below). Required for real-time notifications: without it, every Google Play notification is refused.

Save, then Test connection.

3. Real-time notifications (refunds and renewals)​

  1. In Google Cloud Pub/Sub, create a topic. On the topic's Permissions, give google-play-developer-notifications@system.gserviceaccount.com the Pub/Sub Publisher role, so Google Play can post to it.

  2. Create a push subscription on the topic. Set the endpoint to the Real-time notifications push URL shown on the card:

    https://admin.your-domain.com/api/v1/webhooks/google-play

    Turn on authentication, pick a service account, and set the audience to the same URL.

  3. Enter that service account's email in Pub/Sub push service account on the Google Play card and Save.

  4. In Play Console › Monetize with Play › Monetization setup › Real-time developer notifications, enter the topic's full name (projects/<project>/topics/<topic>) and choose Send test notification.

Google Play notifications are refused without it. Each notification is checked, stored once, and processed in the background (up to 5 tries). Google's voided purchases list is also checked daily, so refunds and chargebacks are never missed.

4. Products​

Create the coin packs (consumable) and the vip subscription with base plans weekly, monthly and yearly, matching the IDs in Monetization. See In-app purchases.

warning

A purchase must be for the product the app names; the server refuses mismatches. VIP base plans must not carry a free-trial offer.
