Welcome to eDrama

eDrama is a complete short-drama streaming business: a mobile app where viewers watch vertical episodes of 60 to 90 seconds, a back office where your team runs the catalogue and the money, and a portal where invited creators publish their own series. The first few episodes of every series are free. After that, viewers unlock an episode with coins, by watching one short ad, with a Series Pass, or with a VIP subscription. Fair, visible pricing is the product's promise, and it is built into the code so nobody can switch it off by accident.

This documentation covers all of it, from installing the server to publishing the app in the stores, and every screen your team, your creators and your viewers will use.

The four parts of eDrama​

Viewer appThe Android and iOS app your audience downloads. Built with Flutter.→Admin Panel (the “Studio”)Your team’s back office at /admin: titles, uploads, creators, users, money and settings.→Creator PortalA second panel at /creator where invited creators upload and manage only their own series.→Public website & APIShare pages, legal pages, the creator application form and the API the app talks to.→

The Admin Panel, the Creator Portal and the API all run on one server (a computer on the internet that you rent from a hosting company). The mobile app is a separate project that you build on your own computer and publish to Google Play and the App Store.

How to read these pages​

  1. Follow the menu paths. A chip like Admin Panel›Settings›Branding tells you exactly where to click: open the panel shown in colour, then each part in turn. Creator Portal paths are gold, such as Creator Portal›My series›New series, and the app's are blue, such as App›Me›Settings.
  2. Use the numbered screens. Most pages show the screen they describe with numbered markers. Each number is explained right below it; point at a number (or tap it) to highlight its explanation.
  3. Watch for the coloured notes. A green Tip saves you time, a blue Note explains why something works the way it does, an amber Warning protects you from a mistake, and a red Danger is about something that can't be undone.
