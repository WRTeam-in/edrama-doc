Permissions and platform settings

The project is already configured for both platforms. This page lists what's there, why each item exists, and the few you change when you rebrand. Use it when you fill in the stores' privacy forms, so your answers match what the app does.

Android​

Versions​

SettingValueWhere
Minimum Android7.0 (API 24)minSdk in android/app/build.gradle.kts
Target AndroidAPI 36 (Flutter's default for 3.47.7)targetSdk (from Flutter)
Java17build.gradle.kts
Code shrinkingR8 on for release (shrink, optimise, obfuscate)build.gradle.kts

Permissions​

The app itself declares two permissions; the libraries it uses add the rest when Android builds the app.

PermissionAdded byWhy
POST_NOTIFICATIONSThe appPushes on Android 13+. Asked once, when the viewer first shows interest, never at launch.
VIBRATEThe appTouch feedback on taps, unlocks and errors (viewers can turn it off).
INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATEFlutter, Firebase, video playerEverything online, and noticing when the connection drops.
WAKE_LOCK, FOREGROUND_SERVICE, com.google.android.c2dm.permission.RECEIVEFirebase Cloud MessagingReceiving pushes.
com.android.vending.BILLINGIn-app purchasesCoin packs and VIP through Google Play.
com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_AD_ID, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_ATTRIBUTION, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_TOPICSGoogle Mobile Ads (AdMob)Rewarded ads. Declare "Yes, my app uses advertising ID" in Play Console.
BIND_GET_INSTALL_REFERRER_SERVICEFirebase AnalyticsWhere an install came from.

The app asks for no camera, location, contacts or storage permission on Android: profile photos are picked through the system's photo picker.

Other settings already in place​

SettingValueWhy
BackupallowBackup="false", no device-to-device copyThe app's data is a cache of your server's, tied to this install; a new phone signs in instead.
Launch modesingleTopTapping a push or a link reuses the open app.
App linksTwo https hosts with autoVerifyShare and website links open the app (Deep links).
Notification defaultsIcon ic_stat_edrama, colour brand_green, channel drops(Push notifications)
AdMob app ID${admobAppId} from admob.appIdFilled in by the build; don't edit the manifest (Ads setup)
Screen protectionFLAG_SECURE while the player is open, when protectScreen is onBlocks screenshots and recordings of episodes (Defaults & limits)

You change: only android:label (the name under the icon) and the two link hosts in build.gradle.kts.

ios/Runner/Info.plist:

KeyValue in the downloadChange it?
CFBundleDisplayNameeDramaYes: the name under the icon
NSPhotoLibraryUsageDescription"Choose a photo for your eDrama profile."Yes: name your app
NSCameraUsageDescription"Take a photo for your eDrama profile."Yes: name your app
NSUserTrackingUsageDescription"This lets eDrama show ads that fit you better…"Yes: name your app. Shown in Apple's tracking prompt (Ads setup)
GADApplicationIdentifierGoogle's test AdMob app IDYes: your AdMob iOS app ID

SKAdNetworkItemsGoogle's ad network IDsNo: AdMob needs them to attribute ads

LSApplicationQueriesSchemesmailto, messageNo: lets Help open the email app

FlutterDeepLinkingEnabledtrueNo: links open the right screen
CFBundleShortVersionString, CFBundleVersionFrom pubspec.yamlNo: change version: in pubspec.yaml instead (Release build)

Entitlements and capabilities​

ios/Runner/Runner.entitlements:

EntitlementValueChange it?
com.apple.developer.associated-domainsapplinks:your-domain.com, applinks:www.your-domain.comYes: your share host and website host (Deep links)
aps-environmentdevelopmentNo: Xcode sets production for App Store builds
com.apple.developer.applesigninDefaultNo: Sign in with Apple
com.apple.developer.devicecheck.appattest-environmentproductionNo: App Check with App Attest

Each needs its capability on your App ID. With automatic signing, Xcode adds them when you pick your team (Signing & certificates).

What this means for the store forms​

Store questionAnswer for eDrama
Google Play › Advertising IDYes, the app uses it (AdMob).
Google Play › Data safety and Apple › App PrivacyCollected: email or phone number (sign-in), user ID, purchase history, app interactions (watching, likes, comments), crash logs and diagnostics (Crashlytics), device or other IDs (ads, push). Encrypted in transit. Viewers can delete their account in the app.
Apple › Export complianceStandard encryption only (already answered by ITSAppUsesNonExemptEncryption = false).
Apple › TrackingYes if you show personalised AdMob ads to iPhone viewers who allow tracking; the app shows Apple's prompt first.

Check these against what you actually turn on (for example, without phone sign-in you don't collect phone numbers), and against your privacy policy.
