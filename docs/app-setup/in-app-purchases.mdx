Viewers buy **coin packs** (one-time purchases) and **VIP** (a subscription) through Google Play and the App Store. The app itself needs no code change: it asks your server which products to show, then the store sells them, and your server checks every receipt before coins land.

What you must do is create the **same products** in each store, with IDs that match the ones in <Path>Money › Monetization</Path>. The **Series Pass** is paid in coins, so it needs no store product.

## Before you start

- Your app exists in Play Console and App Store Connect, with your own [package name](name-and-package.mdx).
- **Google Play:** a **payments profile** is set up for your developer account, and a build with billing has been uploaded to a testing track at least once. Play Console only lets you create products after that.
- **App Store:** the **Paid Applications Agreement** is signed, with your banking and tax details, in App Store Connect › **Business**.

## The product IDs the installer created

You can keep these or change them, as long as the store and the Admin Panel agree.

### Coin packs

| Pack | Google Play product ID | App Store product ID |
|---|---|---|
| 300 + 300 bonus (first top-up offer) | `coins_300_first` | `com.edrama.app.coins.300.first` |
| 500 + 50 bonus | `coins_500` | `com.edrama.app.coins.500` |
| 1000 + 200 bonus (Popular) | `coins_1000` | `com.edrama.app.coins.1000` |
| 2000 + 600 bonus | `coins_2000` | `com.edrama.app.coins.2000` |
| 5000 + 2000 bonus (Best value) | `coins_5000` | `com.edrama.app.coins.5000` |

Create these as **consumable** products (they can be bought again and again).

### VIP

| Plan | Google Play | App Store |
|---|---|---|
| Weekly | Subscription `vip`, base plan `weekly` | `com.edrama.app.vip.weekly` |
| Monthly | Subscription `vip`, base plan `monthly` | `com.edrama.app.vip.monthly` |
| Yearly | Subscription `vip`, base plan `yearly` | `com.edrama.app.vip.yearly` |

Create these as **auto-renewable subscriptions**, step by step below. On iOS, put the three in one subscription group.

:::danger[No free trials]
eDrama's VIP never starts with a free trial or a cut-price introductory offer: it's one of the [fair-play rules](../monetization/fair-play-rules.mdx). The server refuses a subscription the store started with a free trial (and leaves a Google Play one unacknowledged so Google refunds it). **Don't add offers or trials** to these products in either store.
:::

## Create the VIP subscriptions

### Google Play

On Google Play, a **subscription** holds one or more **base plans**, each with its own billing period and prices. eDrama stores both IDs for each VIP plan, so either layout works: **one subscription with three base plans** (subscription `vip`, base plans `weekly`, `monthly`, `yearly`), or three subscriptions with one base plan each.

<Steps>

1. Play Console › your app › **Monetize with Play › Products › Subscriptions** › **Create subscription**. Enter the **Product ID** (such as `vip`; it can't change later) and a name. This is the **Google Play subscription ID** in eDrama.
2. In the subscription, **Add base plan**: **Base plan ID** `weekly` (the **Google Play base plan ID** in eDrama), type **Auto-renewing**, billing period **Weekly**, and the prices per country (Google can fill other countries from one price).
3. **Save**, then **Activate** the base plan. Repeat for `monthly` and `yearly`.
4. **Don't create offers** (free trials or intro prices) on these base plans.

</Steps>

### App Store

On the App Store, subscriptions live in a **subscription group**. A viewer can hold only one subscription of a group at a time and can move between them, so put all three VIP plans in **one group**.

<Steps>

1. App Store Connect › your app › **Monetization › Subscriptions** › **Create** a subscription group, such as `VIP`. Add a group localization (the name viewers see in their subscription settings).
2. In the group, **Create** a subscription for each plan: a **Reference Name** (such as `VIP Weekly`), the **Product ID** (such as `com.yourcompany.drama.vip.weekly`; it can never be reused: this is the **App Store product ID** in eDrama), the **Subscription Duration** (1 Week, 1 Month or 1 Year), the **price**, a **localization** (display name and description) and **Review Information** (a screenshot of the VIP screen and a short note).
3. Drag the three into **levels** in the group, with your best-value plan (often yearly) at the top. Apple uses the order for upgrades and downgrades.
4. Leave **Introductory Offers**, **Promotional Offers** and **Offer Codes** empty.
5. Your first subscriptions are reviewed **with an app version**: select them in the version's **In-App Purchases and Subscriptions** section before you submit it.

</Steps>

### Enter the IDs in the Admin Panel

<Path>Money › Monetization</Path>, pick the region, open **VIP plans** and **Edit** each plan: the **Price** in the region's currency (matching the store price), the **Google Play subscription ID**, the **Google Play base plan ID**, the **App Store product ID**, the **Perks** (one per line) and **Offered in the app**. Do it for every region you sell in: the store IDs are usually the same everywhere, the prices are per region. A price of zero, or an ID or perk that names a trial, is refused.

## Prices

Each store sets its own price tiers per country. Set the store prices to match what you entered in the Admin Panel for each region, as closely as the tiers allow. The coin store in the app lists your Admin Panel prices (with a line such as "Prices in INR · India"), and the store confirms its own price at checkout: if the two differ, viewers notice.

## Connect the stores to your server

The server needs keys to check receipts and to hear about refunds and renewals:

- [Google Play setup](../integrations/google-play.mdx): a service account, and real-time developer notifications.
- [App Store setup](../integrations/app-store.mdx): an In-App Purchase key, and App Store Server Notifications.

## Test before launch

| Store | How | Subscription renewals while testing |
|---|---|---|
| **Google Play** | Add your testers in Play Console › **Settings › License testing**, then install the app from a testing track. | Speeded up: weekly and monthly renew about every 5 minutes, yearly about every 30 minutes. |
| **App Store** | Create a **Sandbox** tester (App Store Connect › **Users and Access › Sandbox**) and sign in with it on a TestFlight or development build. No server setting changes: purchases are checked with the live App Store, then the sandbox. | Speeded up: 1 week ≈ 3 minutes, 1 month ≈ 5 minutes, 1 year ≈ 1 hour. |

Buy a coin pack and check that it appears on <Path>Money › Transactions</Path> with its receipt checks passed. After a few VIP renewals, the test subscription shows on the viewer in <Path>Audience › Users</Path> with **Auto-renew** on.

## How it behaves

- The app never decides how many coins a purchase gives: it sends the store's receipt to your server, which checks it with the store and then adds the coins or turns VIP on.
- Each renewal, cancellation, refund or chargeback reaches eDrama through the stores' **server notifications**, and VIP is extended or ended to match. Set them up in [Google Play](../integrations/google-play.mdx) and [App Store](../integrations/app-store.mdx).
- Viewers get a reminder **24 hours before every renewal charge**, and cancel from the app's VIP screen, which opens the store's own subscription page.
- **Restore purchases** in the coin store claims anything that was interrupted.
