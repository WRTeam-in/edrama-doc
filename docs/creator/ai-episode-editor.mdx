The **Episode editor** in AI Studio turns your kept takes (and videos made outside eDrama) into one episode. You arrange, trim and split pieces on a timeline; nothing ever changes the original files. When it's right, **export**: the finished video goes to the episode through the normal upload pipeline.

## Build the timeline

- **Add kept takes** puts the kept take of every shot not on the timeline yet, in shot order.
- **Drag** a take or imported video onto the timeline: drop on a clip to put it before or after, or in empty space to place it there. The **+** after the last clip adds another video at the end.
- Select a clip to edit it in **Properties**: **In** and **Out** points (set to the playhead with **I** and **O**, or one frame at a time), **Volume**, **Mute this clip**, or **Use another take of this shot**.

## Tools

| Tool | Shortcut | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Split** | S | Splits the clip at the playhead. |
| **Delete** | Delete | Removes the clip and leaves a gap (gaps play as black). |
| **Delete and close the gap** | Shift + Delete | Removes it and moves the rest left. |
| **Ripple** | R | When on, trimming or deleting moves the clips after it. |
| **Snap** | N | Snaps to clip edges and the playhead. |
| **Close gaps** | | Moves every clip left so nothing plays black. |
| **Zoom** | + / − | Zoom in and out; **Fit** shows the whole timeline. |
| **Undo / Redo** | ⌘Z / ⇧⌘Z | |
| **Mute the audio track** | | The export is silent. |

The editor **saves automatically**. If the timeline changed in another tab, it asks you to reload.

## Import a video

**Import video** brings in a video made outside eDrama. It joins the series' library, ready for any episode. Nothing is sent to Google and nothing is charged.

- Confirm you own the video or have permission to use it.
- Limits (defaults): **300 MB**, **30 minutes**, **200** imported videos per series.
- If a file can't be read, export it again as **MP4 (H.264)**.
- You can also **merge an episode's existing video** with new clips, when the original upload is kept on the server.

## Export

**Export** settings: **Frame** (same as the first clip, 720p or 1080p), **Frame rate** (same as the first clip, 24, 25 or 30 fps) and **Quality** (Standard, High, Best). The panel shows the output, such as *1080 × 1920 · 30 fps · 1:32*.

Export waits until every check passes:

- [x] Clips are on the timeline
- [x] Every shot of the episode is on the timeline
- [x] Every clip's file is available
- [x] It's at least **45 seconds** long
- [x] The episode can take a new video (it isn't in review or released)
- [x] The server can render video

If the episode already has a video, you're asked to confirm replacing it. The export goes through **Waiting for the server → Gathering the clips → Rendering → Handing to the video pipeline → Processing as an upload**. When it's done you get a notice: *Episode 3 is assembled*. Then [send it for review](submit-for-review.mdx) as usual.

:::info[Labelled as AI-generated]
Episodes made in AI Studio carry an **AI-generated** label for the review team, with how each shot was made. Every frame also carries Google's **SynthID** watermark.
:::
