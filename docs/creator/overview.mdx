The **Creator Portal** is where invited creators (independent filmmakers, studios, storytellers) publish their own series on your platform. A creator sees **only their own work**: they create series, upload episodes, send them for review, set release times, follow their stats and reviews, see their earnings, and can use AI Studio. It's at `https://your-domain.com/creator`.

This section is written for creators: share its link with yours.

## Turn the portal on (for the platform team)

The portal is part of your Admin Panel's server, at <ServerUrl path="/creator" inline />. Before your first creator signs in:

<Steps>

1. **Add the Firebase web keys.** The portal signs creators in with Firebase in the browser, so it needs the **Web API key** and **Auth domain** in <Path>Settings › Notifications</Path> › **Firebase** ([Firebase](../integrations/firebase.mdx)).
2. **Allow your domain in Firebase.** Firebase console › **Authentication › Settings › Authorized domains** › **Add domain**: your Admin Panel's domain, such as `admin.your-domain.com`.
3. **Turn on the sign-in methods** creators use in the app: Firebase console › **Authentication › Sign-in method** (Google, Email/Password, Phone). Phone sign-in on the portal also follows the **Phone sign-in** switch in <Path>Settings › App & support</Path>.
4. **Publish the Creator Terms** in <Path>Settings › Legal pages</Path>. Creators can't create a series until they're published.
5. **Set the creator limits:** the price range and free episodes in [Payments](../admin/settings/payments-and-revenue.mdx), and the upload limits in [Storage & video](../admin/settings/storage-and-video.mdx) › Video pipeline.

</Steps>

**Check it worked:** open `/creator`. The sign-in page shows the sign-in methods. If it says *Creator sign-in isn't set up yet*, the Firebase web keys are missing. If Firebase says the site isn't allowed, add the domain to **Authorized domains**.

## The menu

**New series** sits above the menu on every page. The menu has two groups, *Overview* and *Your work*:

| Item | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Home** | Your series at a glance and what needs you ([Home](home.mdx)). |
| **Earnings** | Statements and payouts ([Earnings](earnings.mdx)). |
| **My series** | Every series you've made, by status ([My series](series.mdx)). |
| **Upload episodes** | Pick a series and upload ([Upload episodes](upload-episodes.mdx)). |
| **AI Studio** | Characters, storyboards and video takes, when the platform allows it ([AI Studio](ai-studio.mdx)). |
| **Release schedule** | Every episode not out yet, across your series ([Release times](release-times.mdx)). |
| **Comments & reviews** | What viewers say about all your series ([Comments & reviews](comments-and-reviews.mdx)). |
| **Notices** | Review decisions and news from the team ([Notices & account](notices-and-account.mdx)). |

## Good to know

- The portal is in your **own language** if the platform offers it: pick it from the language menu. Your app keeps its own language.
- Times are shown in **your time zone**, which you set in **Your account › Preferences**.
- A session lasts 12 hours. If your account is suspended, you're signed out at your next click.
