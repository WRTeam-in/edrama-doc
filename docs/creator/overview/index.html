The Creator Portal

The Creator Portal is where invited creators (independent filmmakers, studios, storytellers) publish their own series on your platform. A creator sees only their own work: they create series, upload episodes, send them for review, set release times, follow their stats and reviews, see their earnings, and can use AI Studio. It's at https://your-domain.com/creator.

This section is written for creators: share its link with yours.

What creators can do​

Create a seriesDetails, artwork, free episodes and price, regions.→Upload episodesStraight from the browser, with subtitles.→Send for reviewEvery series and episode is checked by the team.→Set release timesPick when each approved episode drops.→Talk to viewersReply to reviews and comments.→See earningsMonthly statements and payouts.→

How it works​

  1. Apply or get invitedThe team accepts you
  2. Accept the termsCreator Terms
  3. Create a seriesUpload its episodes
  4. In reviewThe team checks it
  5. LiveViewers watch it
  6. EarnYour share, monthly

Turn the portal on (for the platform team)​

The portal is part of your Admin Panel's server, at https://admin.your-domain.com/creator. Before your first creator signs in:

  1. Add the Firebase web keys. The portal signs creators in with Firebase in the browser, so it needs the Web API key and Auth domain in Admin Panel›Settings›Notifications › Firebase (Firebase).
  2. Allow your domain in Firebase. Firebase console › Authentication › Settings › Authorized domains › Add domain: your Admin Panel's domain, such as admin.your-domain.com.
  3. Turn on the sign-in methods creators use in the app: Firebase console › Authentication › Sign-in method (Google, Email/Password, Phone). Phone sign-in on the portal also follows the Phone sign-in switch in Admin Panel›Settings›App & support.
  4. Publish the Creator Terms in Admin Panel›Settings›Legal pages. Creators can't create a series until they're published.
  5. Set the creator limits: the price range and free episodes in Payments, and the upload limits in Storage & video › Video pipeline.

Check it worked: open /creator. The sign-in page shows the sign-in methods. If it says Creator sign-in isn't set up yet, the Firebase web keys are missing. If Firebase says the site isn't allowed, add the domain to Authorized domains.

The menu​

New series sits above the menu on every page. The menu has two groups, Overview and Your work:

ItemWhat it's for
HomeYour series at a glance and what needs you (Home).
EarningsStatements and payouts (Earnings).
My seriesEvery series you've made, by status (My series).
Upload episodesPick a series and upload (Upload episodes).
AI StudioCharacters, storyboards and video takes, when the platform allows it (AI Studio).
Release scheduleEvery episode not out yet, across your series (Release times).
Comments & reviewsWhat viewers say about all your series (Comments & reviews).
NoticesReview decisions and news from the team (Notices & account).

Good to know​

  • The portal is in your own language if the platform offers it: pick it from the language menu. Your app keeps its own language.
  • Times are shown in your time zone, which you set in Your account › Preferences.
  • A session lasts 12 hours. If your account is suspended, you're signed out at your next click.
