| Figure | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Views** | How many times episodes were watched. |
| **Unlocks** | Episodes unlocked, by method. |
| **Followers gained** | New followers of the series. |
| **Watched to 90%** | The share of viewers who reached 90% of the episode: your best sign that an episode holds attention. |
| **Went on** | The share who continued to the next episode. A big drop after one episode is worth a look. |

**Your own** viewing, unlocks and follows aren't counted. Numbers refresh every **15 minutes**; the page says when they were last counted.

Money isn't shown here: see [Earnings](earnings.mdx).
