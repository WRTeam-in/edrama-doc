There are two ways in: **apply**, or be **invited** by the team. Either way you end with an invite email.

## Apply

<Steps>

1. Open `https://your-domain.com/creator/apply` (or **Become a creator** in the app's Me tab).
2. Fill in:
   - **Your name**: the name you publish under.
   - **Email**: where the team replies. Use the email you'd like to be invited with.
   - **Links to your past work**: one per line (YouTube, Instagram, a portfolio or a festival page).
   - **Your pitch**: what you'd publish, in a few sentences.
3. Choose **Send application**.

</Steps>

The team watches your work, usually within a few days, and emails you either way. If it's a yes, the email has your link to the Creator Portal.

If the platform isn't taking applications right now, the form says *Applications are closed for now*.

## Accept your invite

The invite email says **Welcome to *platform*, *your name***, with a **Sign in to accept** button. The link works **once, for 7 days**.

<Steps>

1. Open the link. The invite page shows the name you'll publish as and until when the invite is valid.
2. Sign in with the account you use in the app (below). This accepts the invite and makes your creator account **Active**.

</Steps>

| If you see… | It means |
|---|---|
| *This invite link doesn't work* | A newer invite replaced it, or the link is incomplete. Use the newest email, or ask the team to resend. |
| *This invite expired* | Ask the team to send a new one. |
| *This invite was already used* | Sign in to the Creator Portal with the account you used. |
| *A guest account can't become a creator account* | Sign in with Google, your email or your phone number. |
| *This account already belongs to another creator* | Use a different account. |

Sign-in methods: **Google**, **email and password**, or **phone** (when the platform allows it). **Sign in with Apple isn't available in the portal.**

Common messages: *That email and password don't match*, *That code isn't right* (check the SMS), *That code expired* (send a new one), *Your browser blocked the Google window* (allow pop-ups for the site).

If the account you sign in with isn't a creator yet, you see **This isn't a creator account yet**, with a link to apply. If your account is suspended, you see **Your creator account is suspended**, with the platform's support email.

## Accept the Creator Terms

Before you can create a series, you read and accept the **Creator Terms**: a reading page with an outline of its sections, the version and the date it was updated.

<Steps>

1. Read the terms.
2. Tick **I have read and agree to the Creator Terms (version N)**.
3. Choose **Accept and continue**.

</Steps>

When the platform publishes a new version, you're asked to read and accept it again before continuing. Your accepted version and date are on **Your account › Agreement**.
