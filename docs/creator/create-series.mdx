## Details

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Name** | Required. |
| **Subtitle** | A short line under the name on the series page. |
| **Kicker** | A small label above the name on Home. |
| **New episodes** (schedule line) | Such as "New episodes every Friday". |
| **Audio languages** | Press Enter after each. |
| **Synopsis** | Up to 280 characters. It shows **before people pay**, so no spoilers past episode 3. |
| **Categories** | Up to **3**. They decide where viewers find your series. |
| **Age rating** | All, 13+, 16+ or 18+. |
| **Other languages** | Optional translations; empty shows your default text. |

## Artwork

| Image | Size |
|---|---|
| **Poster** (required) | 2:3, at least 600 × 900 |
| **Banner** (optional) | 16:9, at least 960 × 540 |

## Free episodes and price

This is **your proposal**; a reviewer confirms it (and may adjust it within the platform's limits) before the series goes live.

| Field | Rule (defaults) |
|---|---|
| **Free episodes** | At least **5**. Viewers watch these before they need coins. |
| **Coin price per episode** | Between **30** and **90** coins. |

## Where it's shown

- **Every region**: viewers in every country can find and watch it.
- **Only the regions I pick**: when you have the rights only in some countries, or made it for one market. Viewers anywhere else won't find it. Anyone who already unlocked an episode or bought the Series Pass keeps it.

Each region lists the countries it covers. A reviewer confirms your choice; if they change it, you get a notice and an email.

## Rights

Tick: *I own this work or have the rights to publish it, including its music, images and text.* It's required.

Choose **Create series**: it's saved as a **Draft**. Next: [upload its episodes](upload-episodes.mdx).

## Editing a series

How editing works depends on the status:

| Status | Editing |
|---|---|
| **Draft** or **Changes requested** | Edit directly. Changes are saved at once. |
| **Live** | Your changes go to the review team as a **series update**. Viewers keep seeing the series as it is until a reviewer approves. While an update waits, *Update in review* shows, and you can't send another. |
| **In review**, **Approved**, **Rejected** | Can't be changed now. |
| **Unpublished** | Contact the team. |

If the team changes your series' prices, you get a notice and an email with the reason.
