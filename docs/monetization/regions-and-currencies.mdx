## Why regions

A price that's fair in the United States is too high in India. **Regions** let each market have its own currency, coin value, coin packs, VIP plans, title prices, ad limits and rewards, while one server runs them all.

| Region setting | Example (India) |
|---|---|
| Currency | INR (₹ before the amount) |
| Coin value | 1 coin = ₹0.29 |
| Time zone | Asia/Kolkata |
| Countries | India |
| Coin packs | 300 + 300 for ₹99 … |
| VIP | Weekly ₹49, Monthly ₹149, Yearly ₹999 |
| Ads | 20 unlock ads a day |

The **default region** (Global, USD) serves every country no other active region lists.

## How a viewer's region is decided

1. The country the **app store** reports at sign-in.
2. Otherwise the country your **CDN** reports (Cloudflare's `CF-IPCountry` header, from a proxy you trust).
3. Otherwise the default region.

Viewers can't pick their region. Your team can move one ([Users › Change region](../admin/audience/users.mdx)). Purchases, unlocks and price locks always keep the region they were made in.

:::warning[Behind Cloudflare?]
Set `EDRAMA_TRUSTED_PROXIES=cloudflare` so the country header is believed. Without a trusted header, the app's reported country is used (`EDRAMA_TRUST_APP_COUNTRY`), which a determined viewer could change. Monetization › Regions says which source is in use.
:::

## A new region

A new region starts as a **copy** of the default region's packs, plans, prices, ads and rewards, with money prices converted between the two coin values. Review, then turn it on ([Regions](../admin/money/regions.mdx)).

## Reporting in one currency

The Dashboard, Transactions totals, refund limits and creator payouts use one **reporting currency** (USD by default). Each currency needs an **exchange rate** into it, dated from when it applies ([Regions & languages](../admin/settings/regions-and-languages.mdx#reporting-currency-and-exchange-rates)). Rows on days without a rate are **left out and named**, never guessed.

Amounts are stored in each currency's smallest unit (cents, paise), with each currency's own decimals (0 for JPY, 3 for KWD).
