Creator earnings

Creators earn a share (50% by default, set per creator) of what their series bring in. Three streams make up a creator's month.

The three streams​

StreamHow the creator's part is worked out
CoinsPaid coins spent on their episodes and Series Passes (less refunds) × the region's paid coin value × (1 − store fee) × their share. The share saved with each unlock is used, so a later share change never rewrites the past. Worked example below.
AdsThe region's ads pool (unlock-ad earnings from AdMob × the ads pool percent) × their verified ad unlocks ÷ all verified ad unlocks in the region × their share.
VIPThe region's VIP pool (VIP charges less refunds and store fees × the VIP pool percent) × their VIP minutes on locked episodes ÷ all VIP minutes in the region × their share.

Not counted: bonus coins, the creator's own viewing and unlocks, accounts under a day old (for ads), free and YouTube episodes, and granted episodes.

Your own and licensed titles count in each region's totals, so their part of the pools stays with the platform. Licensed titles have their own licensor share.

From a coin to money​

Only paid coins earn money. Bonus coins (the welcome bonus, coin-pack bonuses, the spin, streak titles, Rewards ads) are given away, so they earn nobody anything. For each series, each region and each month:

The coins part of a statement
paid coins     = paid coins spent on its episodes and Series Passes − paid coins taken back by refunds
gross money    = paid coins × paid coin value
net money      = gross money × (1 − store fee ÷ 100)
creator money  = net money × the creator's share ÷ 100
your money     = net money − creator money
TermWhat it means
Coin valueWhat one coin is worth in the region's currency (0.0099 USD in Global and 0.29 INR in India to start), set in Admin Panel›Money›Monetization›Regions.
Paid coin valueWhat one paid coin really brought in that month in the region: the store money of completed purchases divided by every paid coin added (team adjustments included). It's never above the coin value, so discounted packs, test purchases or coins your team added never pay out more than you received. When every purchase paid full price, it equals the coin value.
Store feeGoogle Play's and the App Store's cut (15% each to start, in Admin Panel›Settings›Payments). The fees saved on that month's purchases in the region are used, weighted by amount.
Saved at the timeEach unlock saves the share in force when it happened; each purchase saves its store fee. Changing a setting never rewrites past money.
RefundsWhen a store refund or chargeback arrives after the coins were spent, what those coins earned is taken back in the month of the refund.

Example. A creator series priced at 60 coins in the Global region. In one month, viewers unlock 1,000 episodes with paid coins, all from full-price packs:

Paid coins1,000 × 60 = 60,000
Gross money60,000 × 0.0099 = 594.00 USD
Store fees (15%)89.10 USD
Net money504.90 USD
Creator (50%)252.45 USD
You252.45 USD

What the calculator leaves out

Refunds, discounted packs (which lower the paid coin value), and the ads and VIP pools, which are shared separately (above). It also leaves out your own running costs: hosting, video storage and streaming, taxes and payout fees.

The monthly cycle​

  1. Month ends
  2. StatementCoins share, closed on the 1st
  3. Creator revenueAds & VIP, imported on the 5th
  4. Approve
  5. PayRecorded as a payout
  1. On the 1st, the month before is closed: each creator's statement gets their coin share (minus refunds). The monthly creator report is also built.
  2. On the import day (the 5th by default), Creator revenue imports AdMob's earnings and calculates the ads and VIP pools and each creator's part.
  3. Your team reviews, sets a pool by hand or adjusts a creator if needed, and approves. The ads, VIP and adjustments go onto the statements.
  4. Finance pays each creator outside the app and records the payout (amount, method, reference, date) on the creator's page.

Creators follow the same figures on their Earnings page: earned, paid, waiting to be paid, and this month so far.

Monthly creator report​

Transactions › Monthly creator report (also built automatically each month) gives one row per series and region: paid coins spent, Series Pass coins, ad unlocks and VIP minutes, with the settings used. Useful for licensors and auditing.

To confirm

Earlier project notes said the Creator Portal shows no money figures and payouts are manual. The current code adds the Earnings page and Creator revenue workflow described here; payouts are still made outside the app and recorded by hand.
