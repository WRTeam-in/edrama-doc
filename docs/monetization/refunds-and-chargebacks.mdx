## Three ways a refund happens

| Source | How it arrives |
|---|---|
| **Admin Panel** | A member refunds a Google Play purchase from [Transactions](../admin/money/transactions.mdx#refund-a-purchase). |
| **Store notification** | Google or Apple tells your server about a refund or chargeback. |
| **Google Play voided purchases** | A daily check of Google's list of refunded and charged-back purchases, which always appear there. |

App Store refunds are always decided by **Apple**: the viewer requests them from Apple, and Apple's notification updates the purchase.

## What happens to the coins

A refund can **take the coins back** (the default for store refunds; optional from the panel):

- Coins still in the wallet are taken back as a new **reversal** row in the ledger.
- Coins already spent can't be taken back: the balance **never goes below zero**. The part already spent is recorded on the refund as the **shortfall**.
- If the store later **reverses a refund**, the coins come back.

From the panel, you can also refund the money and **let the viewer keep the coins** (for example, a goodwill refund).

## Who can refund

Each role has a **refund limit** in the reporting currency (Admin 100.00, Finance & Support 20.00 by default; other roles none). Over the limit, someone with a higher limit must do it. The Owner can refund any amount. The panel asks Google first and writes nothing if Google refuses.

## Effects on reporting

- The Dashboard's revenue and Transactions' totals subtract refunds for the days they affect; the daily counts are redone for those days.
- A creator's statement subtracts refunds of coins that were spent on their series; if a refund takes back more than a later month earned, it comes off the next statements.

## Store notifications must be set up

Without Google Play's real-time notifications and App Store Server Notifications, refunds made in the stores reach your server late (Google's daily check) or not at all (Apple). Set them up as described in [Google Play](../integrations/google-play.mdx) and [App Store](../integrations/app-store.mdx). If a notification can't be processed after 5 tries, members with Transactions (Edit) get a bell, and the job can be retried from <Path>System › System</Path>.
