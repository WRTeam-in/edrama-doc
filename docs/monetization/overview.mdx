eDrama earns the way short-drama apps do, with one difference: **fair, visible pricing is built in** and locked, because viewers who trust the price keep coming back.

## The six sources

| Source | How it earns | Configure it |
|---|---|---|
| **Coin packs** | Viewers buy coins in Google Play or the App Store. The server checks every receipt before coins land. | [Coin packs](../admin/money/coin-packs-and-vip.mdx) |
| **Coin unlocks** | Viewers spend coins to unlock an episode, at the series' price. | [Title editor › Prices](../admin/content/title-editor.mdx#prices) |
| **Series Pass** | Viewers spend coins once to unlock every episode of a series, including future ones. | Title editor › Prices |
| **VIP** | A weekly, monthly or yearly store subscription: every episode, no ads. Never a free trial. | [VIP plans](../admin/money/coin-packs-and-vip.mdx#vip-plans) |
| **Rewarded ads** | A viewer watches one ad (30 seconds at most) to unlock an episode or earn coins. AdMob pays you. | [Ads](../admin/money/ads-and-rewards.mdx) |
| **Free episodes** | The first episodes of every series. They earn nothing directly, but they're the hook. | Title editor › Paywall |

## Coins: the app's currency

- **Paid coins** come from purchases and **never expire**.
- **Bonus coins** come from pack bonuses, the welcome bonus, the daily spin, streak titles, ads and goodwill credits. They **expire** (7 days by default) and are **always spent first**, earliest expiry first.
- A coin's money value is set **per region** (for example $0.0099 in the Global region), so prices are fair in every currency. See [Regions & currencies](regions-and-currencies.mdx).

## Where money goes

Each unlock and purchase records the **share** in force at that moment (creator or licensor share, store fee), so later changes never rewrite the past. Creators are paid monthly: their coin share, plus their part of the ad and VIP pools. See [Creator earnings](creator-earnings.mdx).
