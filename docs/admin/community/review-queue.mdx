## What lands in the queue

| Type | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Series** | A creator's new series, sent with its ready episodes. |
| **Episodes** | New episodes of a creator's live series, each on its own. |
| **Series updates** | Proposed changes to a live series' details. The series stays live as it is until you approve. |
| **Reports** | Problem reports from viewers in the player. |

## Take an item

Choose **Assign to me** so two reviewers don't watch the same item. If someone else holds it and can't finish, **Take over** (recorded in the audit log). Keyboard shortcuts are listed at the top: move between items, approve and reject.

Aim for a decision within **24 hours**: items turn **amber after 18 hours** and **red after 24**.

## Decide a series or episode

| Decision | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Approve** | A **series**: confirm the **free episodes** and the **coin price per episode** (both within the limits in <Path>Settings › Payments › Creator price limits</Path>) and **where it's shown**. It goes live at its release time, or now if none is set. An **episode**: it goes live at its release time, or now. |
| **Request changes** | Your reason (up to 1,000 characters) goes to the creator, who can fix it and send it again. |
| **Reject** | Your reason goes to the creator. It won't go live. |
| **Unpublish** | Takes a live series or episode out of the app at once, with a reason. Viewers who unlocked it keep it. |

For a **series update**, a table compares each changed field, **Now** and **Proposed**: name, subtitle, kicker, schedule line, audio languages, synopsis, categories, age rating, poster, banner, free episodes, coin price and regions. Approving applies them; requesting changes or rejecting leaves the series as it is.

If you change where a creator's series is shown, the creator gets a notice and an email saying where.

Every decision leaves a notice in the creator's portal and sends them an email, and is recorded in the audit log. **Earlier decisions** on the same work are listed under each item. A reviewer can't approve a title they own as a creator.

:::tip[Check the rights declaration]
Every creator submission records a **rights declaration**: when it was made, from which address, and the declaration version. If an item has none, ask the creator to declare their rights before you approve.
:::

## Resolve a problem report

A report shows the reason, the episode, the moment in the episode, the device and app version, the viewer's message, and other reports on the same episode.

| Action | Use it when |
|---|---|
| **Mark resolved** | You fixed or checked it. Write a resolution note (such as "re-encoded E14 and playback works again"). The note stays with the team. |
| **Ask the creator to fix it** | It's a creator's episode. They get your reason in the portal and by email; the episode stays live. The report is resolved. |
| **Unpublish this episode** | Viewers shouldn't see it meanwhile. |
