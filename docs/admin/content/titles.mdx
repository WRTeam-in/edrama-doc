## The columns

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Title** | Poster, name and how many episodes are out (such as "8 of 24 out"). |
| **Owner** | eDrama Original, Licensed (with the licensor) or Creator (with the creator). |
| **Episodes** | How many episodes exist. |
| **Access** | How many are free ("5 free"), or **No price** if no price is set yet. |
| **Status** | Where the title stands (below). |
| **Views · Revenue · Completion** | The last 30 days for the region in the top bar. *Completion* is the share of viewers who reached 90% of an episode. |

If some days have no exchange rate into your reporting currency, a note above the table names the currencies left out of **Revenue**.

Each row's **More** menu jumps straight to a part of the editor: **Edit details**, **Episodes**, **Pricing & paywall** or **Localization**.

## Who owns a title

Every title has an **owner type**, which decides who shares its revenue:

| Owner type | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **eDrama Original** | Made and owned by you. All revenue stays with you. |
| **Licensed** | Licensed from a studio or distributor. You name the **licensor** and, once the contract is set, their share of net revenue. |
| **Creator** | A series by an invited creator, who earns their share of every unlock. Creator series normally arrive from the [Creator Portal](../../creator/overview.mdx). |

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Draft** | Being prepared. Not visible in the app. |
| **In review** | Waiting for a reviewer's decision. |
| **Changes requested** | Sent back to its owner with a reason. |
| **Approved** | Checked and ready to publish. |
| **Live** | In the app now. |
| **Rejected** | Won't go live. |
| **Unpublished** | Taken out of the app. Viewers keep what they unlocked. |

Your team's titles are approved in the [title editor](title-editor.mdx) by a member with **Review queue** (Edit). A creator's series is decided only in the [Review queue](../community/review-queue.mdx).

## Create a title

<Steps>

1. Choose **New title**.
2. Type its name (such as *The Heiress Returns*) and choose **Create draft**.
3. The editor opens. Add details and artwork, upload the episodes, and set the free episodes and prices.
4. Choose **Submit for review**, then **Approve** and **Publish** (with the right permissions).

</Steps>

Every title starts as a draft. Before a title can go live, it needs:

- at least one episode whose video is ready,
- a coin price in the **default region**,
- no more free episodes than it has episodes.

To add many titles at once, use **Import › New titles** ([Bulk imports](bulk-imports.mdx)).

## Delete a draft

Only a **draft that no viewer has paid for** can be deleted: open it and choose **Delete draft**. Anything else is unpublished instead, so viewers' unlocks and the money records stay intact.
