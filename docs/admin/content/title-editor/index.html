The title editor

Open any title from Admin Panel›Content›Titles to edit it. The editor has four tabs: Overview, Episodes, Monetization and Localization.

Overview tab​

Details​

FieldWhat it's for
TitleThe name viewers see.
SlugThe title's address in the app and in share links. It becomes fixed once the title is published, because links use it.
SubtitleA short line under the name on the series page.
KickerA small label above the name on Home, such as "New this week".
Release schedule lineWhen new episodes come out, such as "New episodes every Friday".
Audio languagesThe languages it can be heard in. Press Enter after each.
SynopsisUp to 280 characters. No spoilers past episode 3: it shows before people pay.
CategoriesDrive Home rows, Browse and search. Creator titles pick up to 3.
Age ratingAll, 13+, 16+ or 18+.
CompleteTells viewers every episode is out: the app shows "Complete" instead of the next drop. It can be turned on only once every episode is released, and a new episode turns it off.

Artwork​

ImageSizeFormats
Poster2:3, at least 600 × 900 (1200 × 1800 is best)JPG, PNG or WebP, up to 5 MB
Banner16:9, at least 960 × 540 (1920 × 1080 is best)JPG, PNG or WebP, up to 5 MB

The app crops each one for where it's shown.

Ownership & rights​

Choose the Owner (eDrama Original, Licensed or Creator). For a licensed title, name the Licensor and their Licensor share of net revenue once the contract is set. For a creator title, pick the Creator; their share shows here ("Name gets 50% of net revenue"). Add a Rights note if useful.

Once a title has been published, changing its owner asks for a reason: its revenue records depend on who owns it.

Other languages​

Under Other languages, a tab per app language holds translations of the name, subtitle, kicker, schedule line and synopsis. Leave a box empty to show the default language's text. To translate many titles at once, use Import › Translations on the Titles list.

Comments & reviews​

A link to what viewers say about this title, on Comments & reviews.

Status actions​

The buttons at the top change with the title's status.

ButtonWhenWhat happens
Submit for reviewDraft or Changes requestedA reviewer checks the episodes, artwork and prices.
ApproveIn review (your team's titles)Needs Review queue (Edit). The title can then be published.
Request changesIn reviewSends it back with your reason. It needs a new review before publishing.
PublishApproved or UnpublishedReleased episodes appear in the app for every region with a price.
UnpublishLiveAsks for a reason. The title disappears from Home, Browse and search; viewers keep the episodes they unlocked.
Delete draftDraft, never paid forRemoves it for good.

Episodes tab​

Lists every episode with its length, access, status, processing, subtitles and release time. See Episodes & subtitles.

Monetization tab​

Paywall​

SettingMeaning
Free episodesHow many episodes from the start anyone can watch. The paywall sits at the next one. It can't be more than the number of episodes.
Included in VIPVIP members watch every episode without coins.
Ads can unlockViewers may watch one ad (30 seconds or less) per episode instead of paying. Each region's daily ad limit applies.

Where it's shown​

Every region (the default), or Only these regions when you only have the rights in some places. Viewers elsewhere no longer find it in Home, search, categories or creator pages, and links to it say it isn't available in their region. Viewers who already unlocked an episode or bought its Series Pass keep it.

note

Your public website shows only what the default region's viewers see.

Prices​

Choose Change prices to set, per region:

  • Coin price per episode,
  • Series Pass (optional): one price that unlocks every episode, including future ones.

A region without its own price uses the default region's. Every price change needs a reason, kept in the audit log with the old and new prices.

Price lock

Viewers who already started the series keep the price they saw: a rise doesn't reach them, a drop does. The dialog shows how many viewers have locked a price for this title. See Coins & pricing.

What viewers see​

A live preview of the cost card on the series page, for the region you pick: which episodes are free, the price per episode ("60 coins an episode, never more"), the ad option, the Series Pass and how many released episodes are left to unlock.

Fair-play locks​

A reminder of the rules that can't be switched off per title: the price lock, unlocks never charge twice, and auto-unlock is off unless the viewer turns it on.

To confirm

This card also lists "A failed rewarded ad still unlocks the episode". The server code does not do this: an ad unlocks an episode only once AdMob's server-side verification confirms it was watched to the end (a failed ad unlocks nothing but still counts toward the daily limit). The Ads tab in Monetization describes the code's behaviour correctly. Ask WRTeam which wording is intended.

Localization tab​

Subtitles, one file per episode and language. See Episodes & subtitles.
