**Website** edits your public marketing website: a home page built from sections, plus automatic pages for every series, category and creator. You change a **draft**, check it in the live preview, and **Publish** when you're happy. Visitors see the change within a minute.

<Access area="Website" view="See the editor" edit="Change and publish the website" />

:::info[Setting the website up]
The website itself is a separate Next.js project that you host (on Vercel or your own Node.js server) and connect to your server. Deploying and connecting it is covered in [Website setup](../../website/overview.mdx); this page covers editing its content.
:::

## The tabs

| Tab | What you set |
|---|---|
| **Sections** | The home page, top to bottom (below). |
| **Header & footer** | Header style (Floating bar, Classic full width, Centred logo, Minimal with menu), menu links, header button, an announcement bar; footer style (Columns, Big name, Compact, Download first), about line, link columns, legal pages, store buttons, contact email and copyright line. |
| **Style** | Theme (Midnight dark, Paper light, or follow the visitor's device), whether visitors can switch light/dark, headings (editorial serif italic or modern), corners and motion (Full, Subtle, None). |
| **Get the app** | Store buttons (from your App versions store links), an optional direct APK download link with its version and minimum Android, and a QR code for computers. |
| **Search & stats** | Site title and title pattern, description, share picture (1200 × 630), whether search engines and AI assistants may read the site, pages for every series, category and creator, `llms.txt`, Google Search Console and Bing codes, and per-page titles and descriptions. |
| **Social & analytics** | Social links (Instagram, YouTube, X, Facebook, TikTok, Threads, LinkedIn, Telegram, WhatsApp, Discord) and visitor statistics (Plausible, Umami or Google Analytics). |
| **Languages** | Which app languages the website offers, a language menu in the header, and translations of every text. |
| **Connection** | Where the website lives and how it talks to your server (below). |
| **History** | Every published version. Load one into the draft, check it, publish it again. |

Your **name, logos and theme colour** come from <Path>Settings › Branding</Path>, so the app, the panels and the website always match.

## Section types

| Section | What it shows | Styles |
|---|---|---|
| **Top slider** | Big pictures with your headline and buttons: your most popular series automatically, or slides you make. | Cinematic full width, Poster stack, Split |
| **Numbers** | Live series, released episodes, categories, creators, app languages, or numbers you type. | Band, Split |
| **Series showcase** | A row or grid of series: new, popular this week, Top 10 today, complete, one category, one creator, or ones you pick. | Row of posters, Large posters, Top 10 with big numbers, Grid, One big and a list, Wide cards |
| **What is it?** | What your app is, with feature points and phone pictures. | Text and phone, Editorial, Centred |
| **Categories** | Your categories with artwork, leading to each category's page. | Artwork tiles, Chips, Mosaic |
| **Get the app** | Store buttons, a direct APK link and a QR code. Always on the page. | Phone showcase, Band, Glowing card |
| **Creators** | Creators with live series, and an invitation to become one. | Avatars, Cards with posters |
| **Questions** | Questions and short answers (search engines and AI assistants read these too). | Accordion, Two columns |
| **Call to action** | A bold band with a heading and a button. | Band, Split, Glowing card |
| **Text block** | A heading, paragraphs and an optional picture. | Text only, Text and picture |

Each section also has a **background** (page colour, raised panel, accent glow or accent colour), **spacing** (compact, regular, airy), an optional **section address** for header links, and a feature condition (**Only while** VIP is on, Rewards are on, and so on) so a section disappears when you switch that feature off. Put `*stars*` around a word or two in a heading to set them in the accent style.

While your catalogue is small, **sample series** can fill series sections until they have 4 live series. Turn this off when your catalogue is ready.

## Connect the website

The website is a separate site. On the **Connection** tab:

<Steps>

1. Copy **This server's address for the website** (`EDRAMA_API_URL`) into the website's settings.
2. Choose **Make a secret** and paste the **Refresh secret** into the website's `REVALIDATE_SECRET`. It's shown only once; **Make a new secret** replaces it.
3. Enter the **Website address** (such as `https://www.your-domain.com`) and choose **Save address**. It's used for previews and to tell the website about changes.

</Steps>

## Publish, preview, switch off

- **Save draft** keeps your changes without publishing. **Discard draft** goes back to the live version.
- **Preview** opens the saved draft on your website for 30 minutes, marked "Preview · not published".
- **Publish** shows the changes to visitors within a minute.
- **Switch website off** shows visitors a short "back soon" page and asks search engines not to index it.

The status above the tabs reads *Live · version N*, *Unpublished changes* or *Everything is published*.
