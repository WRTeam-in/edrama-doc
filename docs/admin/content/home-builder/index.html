Home builder

The app's Home tab is built from sections you arrange here: a hero carousel at the top, Continue Watching, rows and grids of series, banners, category chips, notes and promo cards. A phone preview updates as you work, and nothing reaches viewers until you Publish.

Permission area: Schedule & Home
  • View See the layout
  • Edit Change and publish it

Section types​

TypeWhat it showsStyles
Hero carouselBig swipeable cards for up to 5 live titles you pick, at the top of Home. Can move on by itself every 4, 6, 8 or 10 seconds.Carousel, Spotlight, Billboard, Slim, Clean
Continue watchingEach viewer's series in progress, with the next episode.Cards, Compact, Posters, Progress rings
Series rowA row of series that scrolls sideways.Posters, Large posters, Landscape, Ranked (Top 10), Circles, Detailed, Title on poster, Compact
Series gridSeries in columns, a list or a mosaic.3 columns, 2 columns, 4 columns, List, Mosaic, Landscape
BannerAn image that opens a series, a category, the store, VIP, Rewards or a web link.Wide (16:9), Strip (3:1), Tall (4:5), Edge to edge, Square, Picture beside text
Category chipsCategories viewers tap to browse.Chips, Tiles, Circles, Chips all showing, Tile grid
NoteA short line of text, such as your fair-pricing promise.Plain, Highlight, Callout, Ticker, Solid
Promo cardA heading, a line of text and a button.Tinted, Accent, Outline, Centred, With picture

Home can have up to 30 sections, with one hero and one Continue watching at most.

Where a row's series come from​

Series rows and grids take their titles from a source:

SourceShows
Titles you pickUp to 30 live titles, in the order you drag them.
Because you like…Series from the categories the viewer picked in onboarding. The heading reads "Because you like Revenge".
One categorySeries from the category you choose ("Best of Rebirth").
New releasesNewest first.
Complete seriesSeries with every episode out ("Complete: binge to the end").
Top 10 todayThe top 10 in the viewer's region, recounted every 15 minutes.
Trending this weekRising in the viewer's region.
Most popularThe most watched overall.

Choose how many Titles shown and whether to Show "See all", which opens the full list in the app.

Section settings​

SettingMeaning
Heading / SubtitleLeave the heading empty to use the standard one in the viewer's own language.
StyleHow the section looks (table above).
Image (banners)JPEG, PNG or WebP, up to 2 MB, in the shape of the style: 16:9 (1920 × 1080), 3:1 (1800 × 600), 4:5 (1080 × 1350) or 1:1 (1080 × 1080).
Opens (banners, promos)A series, a category, or a web link; promos and banners can also open the store, VIP or Rewards.
Text / Button label (notes, promos)Up to 280 characters; a button label up to 32.
Show on HomeOn or off.
AudienceEveryone, Guests only, Signed-in viewers, VIP members, or Viewers without VIP.
RegionsLeave empty for every region.
Show from / Show untilAn optional time window, in the default region's time zone. Shows as Scheduled in the list.

A section that would be empty for a viewer is left out, so Home never shows a blank row.

Publish​

  1. Make your changes. The list says Unpublished changes.
  2. Choose Publish layout, then Publish.

The app shows the new layout the next time viewers open Home. The layout gets a new version number only if something changed; publishing an identical layout tells you there's nothing to publish. Each publish is recorded in the audit log.

warning

If a title in the hero was unpublished, it shows Not live: viewers no longer see it there. Remove it before you can publish the layout.
