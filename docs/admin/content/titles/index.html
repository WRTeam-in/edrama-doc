Titles

A title is one series in your catalogue. Titles lists all of them, whoever made them: your own originals, dramas licensed from a studio, and creators' series all share one content model.

Permission area: Titles & episodes
  • View See the list and open titles
  • Edit Create, edit, approve, publish and unpublish titles

The columns​

ColumnMeaning
TitlePoster, name and how many episodes are out (such as "8 of 24 out").
OwnereDrama Original, Licensed (with the licensor) or Creator (with the creator).
EpisodesHow many episodes exist.
AccessHow many are free ("5 free"), or No price if no price is set yet.
StatusWhere the title stands (below).
Views · Revenue · CompletionThe last 30 days for the region in the top bar. Completion is the share of viewers who reached 90% of an episode.

If some days have no exchange rate into your reporting currency, a note above the table names the currencies left out of Revenue.

Each row's More menu jumps straight to a part of the editor: Edit details, Episodes, Pricing & paywall or Localization.

Who owns a title​

Every title has an owner type, which decides who shares its revenue:

Owner typeMeaning
eDrama OriginalMade and owned by you. All revenue stays with you.
LicensedLicensed from a studio or distributor. You name the licensor and, once the contract is set, their share of net revenue.
CreatorA series by an invited creator, who earns their share of every unlock. Creator series normally arrive from the Creator Portal.

Title statuses​

  1. Draft
  2. In review
  3. Approved
  4. Live
  5. Unpublished

A title in review can also be sent back (Changes requested) or Rejected. A live title can be unpublished and published again.

StatusMeaning
DraftBeing prepared. Not visible in the app.
In reviewWaiting for a reviewer's decision.
Changes requestedSent back to its owner with a reason.
ApprovedChecked and ready to publish.
LiveIn the app now.
RejectedWon't go live.
UnpublishedTaken out of the app. Viewers keep what they unlocked.

Your team's titles are approved in the title editor by a member with Review queue (Edit). A creator's series is decided only in the Review queue.

Create a title​

  1. Choose New title.
  2. Type its name (such as The Heiress Returns) and choose Create draft.
  3. The editor opens. Add details and artwork, upload the episodes, and set the free episodes and prices.
  4. Choose Submit for review, then Approve and Publish (with the right permissions).

Every title starts as a draft. Before a title can go live, it needs:

  • at least one episode whose video is ready,
  • a coin price in the default region,
  • no more free episodes than it has episodes.

To add many titles at once, use Import › New titles (Bulk imports).

Delete a draft​

Only a draft that no viewer has paid for can be deleted: open it and choose Delete draft. Anything else is unpublished instead, so viewers' unlocks and the money records stay intact.
