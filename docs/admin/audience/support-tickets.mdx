## Topics

| Topic in the app | Arrives as |
|---|---|
| A refund | Refund |
| Coins didn't arrive | Missing coins |
| Video or playback | Playback |
| My account | Account |
| Something else | Something else |

For refund and missing-coins requests, the viewer can pick **which purchase** it's about; the ticket links to it.

## Handle a ticket

<Steps>

1. Open the ticket. You see the viewer (or "No account"), the purchase, the app version, and the message.
2. Reply to the viewer from your support mailbox, to the **Reply to** address.
3. Set the **Status** and add **Team notes** (viewers never see them), then **Save**.

</Steps>

| Status | Use it for |
|---|---|
| **Open** | New, waiting for a first look. |
| **Pending** | Waiting on someone: the viewer, a store, a developer. |
| **Resolved** | Answered. |
| **Closed** | Nothing more to do. |

The app promises a reply **within 24 hours** (change it with `EDRAMA_SUPPORT_REPLY_HOURS`). Members who can see tickets get a bell notification for each new one.

For a refund, open the purchase from the ticket and follow [Transactions › Refund](../money/transactions.mdx#refund-a-purchase).
