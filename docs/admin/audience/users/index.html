Users

Users are your app's viewers. Find anyone by name, email, phone, user ID or a store order ID, and open their page to see everything about their account.

Permission area: Users
  • View See users (contact details masked)
  • Edit See full contact details, grant episodes, change region, ban and unban

Finding someone​

  • Email or phone: type it in full: search finds an exact match (contact details are stored encrypted, so partial matches aren't possible).
  • User ID: the number on their page.
  • Store order ID: the Google Play or App Store order from a receipt the viewer sent you.
  • Name: the start of their name. Only the first matches are shown; type more to narrow it down.
Privacy

Email addresses and phone numbers are masked unless you have Users (Edit), and each time someone sees them in full it's recorded in the audit log ("Viewed users' contact details").

The user's page​

The top shows the user at a glance: balance, lifetime spend, VIP (and until when), unlocks, last active and sign-in method, plus their user ID, Firebase ID, devices and, if they're also a creator, their creator name.

TabWhat's there
Wallet & ledgerTotal, paid and bonus coins (bonus is spent first; when the next bonus coins expire), and a reconcile check that the balance equals the sum of its ledger rows. Then the ledger: every coin movement, newest first, with the balance after it. Open a row's Details to see what the viewer sees, who made it, and what it was for (the purchase, unlock, refund or Series Pass).
UnlocksEvery episode they can watch and how: coins, ad, Series Pass, VIP, free or grant, with coins used.
PurchasesCoin pack purchases with store, product, amount, coins credited (paid + bonus), status and the receipt check.
VIPEvery subscription: plan, store, status, next charge, auto-renew and start date.
ProgressWhere they stopped in each episode, and whether they finished it.
ActivitySign-ins (time, method, device, IP address) and devices (platform, app version, OS version, last seen).
note

Ledger rows are never edited or deleted. A correction is always a new row. If a wallet doesn't reconcile, nothing is changed: report it to your developer.

Actions​

ActionPermissionWhat it does
Adjust balanceBalance adjustments (Edit)Adds or removes paid or bonus coins with a reason the viewer sees. Over the approval limit, a second member must approve. See Balance adjustments.
Grant episodeUsers (Edit)Gives access to released episodes of a live title with no coins moving and no revenue share, for example after a playback outage. Episodes already unlocked are skipped. Needs a reason.
Change regionUsers (Edit)Moves the viewer to another active region: coin packs, VIP plans and prices follow it from their next app start. Their coins, purchases, unlocks, Series Passes and locked prices stay as they are. A viewer who has paid needs the extra tick Move a viewer who has paid.
Ban accountUsers (Edit)Their sign-in stops working at once; the app stops working for them on their next request. Purchases, ledger and unlocks stay. Needs a reason.
Unban accountUsers (Edit)They can sign in again with everything they had. Needs a reason.

Guests​

A guest is a viewer who hasn't signed in (shown as "Guest #1234"). Guests get the welcome bonus and can watch and earn, but must sign in to buy coins or VIP. When a guest signs in, their history and coins merge into the account; the guest's page then says Merged into with a link.

Deleted accounts​

Viewers can delete their account in the app. Their comment and review text, support ticket contact details and report text are removed. The page shows Account deleted.
