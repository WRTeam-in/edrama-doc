## Coin packs

A **coin pack** is what viewers buy in the store: a number of coins, often with **bonus coins** on top, for a price.

India starts with the same coins at ₹99, ₹199, ₹399, ₹799 and ₹1,999 (placeholders).

### Pack fields

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Coins** / **Bonus coins** | Paid coins never expire; bonus coins expire after the days set on Rewards and are always spent first. |
| **Price** | In the region's currency. |
| **Google Play product ID** | Lower-case letters, digits, dots, underscores and dashes. Must match the product in Play Console. |
| **App Store product ID** | Must match the product in App Store Connect. |
| **Shown in the app** | Off hides the pack without deleting it. |
| **Order** | The order in the store. |
| **First top-up** / **Popular** / **Best value** | Only one pack per region can carry each flag. The first top-up offer can be bought **once per account**. |

See [In-app purchases](../../app-setup/in-app-purchases.mdx) for creating the products in each store.

:::note
A pack someone has bought can't be deleted, so it stays in their history: turn off **Shown in the app** instead. A pack with a pending purchase can't be deleted either.
:::

## VIP plans

**VIP** is a subscription that unlocks every drama, with no ads. Each region has three plans: **Weekly**, **Monthly** and **Yearly**.

| Plan | Global | India (placeholder) |
|---|---|---|
| Weekly | $5.99 | ₹49 |
| Monthly | $19.99 | ₹149 |
| Yearly | $119.99 | ₹999 |

Each plan card shows its price, the weekly equivalent and saving, subscribers and how many have auto-renew on. **Edit** a plan to change:

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Price** | Above zero. |
| **Offered in the app** | Off hides the plan. |
| **Google Play subscription ID** and **base plan ID** | The base plan itself, never an offer. |
| **App Store product ID** | |
| **Perks** | One per line, as the app lists them. Default: *Every drama unlocked. No ads.* |

:::danger[No free trials]
VIP has **no free trial**: the price must be above zero, and no ID or perk may name a trial or a free introductory offer. The server refuses a subscription that started with a free trial.
:::

### Rules in the app

The VIP page in the app lists the subscription's terms in plain words. By default it shows the **built-in rules**, translated into every app language. Choose **Edit rules** to write your own (one per line, per language), or **Use built-in rules** to go back.

### Locked subscription rules

These can't be switched off:

- No free trial that turns into a charge.
- A renewal reminder **24 hours** before every charge.
- Cancel in two taps, in the app.
- VIP never sees an ad.

See [VIP subscriptions](../../monetization/vip-subscriptions.mdx).
