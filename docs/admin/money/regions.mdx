**Monetization** is where you set what things cost. It has six tabs: **Regions**, **Coin packs**, **VIP plans**, **Ads**, **Rewards** and **Fair-play rules**. Everything except Regions and Fair-play rules is set **per region**: pick the region with the **Region** switch at the top of the page.

<Access area="Prices & coin packs" view="See every tab" edit="Change regions, packs, plans, ads and rewards" />

A **region** is a group of countries that share a currency and prices. Selling the same drama at fair local prices in, say, India and the United States is what regions are for.

## How a viewer's region is chosen

A viewer's region comes from their country: the country their app store reports at sign-in, otherwise the country your CDN reports (Cloudflare's `CF-IPCountry` header), otherwise the default region. A note above the table says which source your server is using. A viewer **can't choose** another region; your team can move one in [Users › Change region](../audience/users.mdx).

## Add a region

<Steps>

1. Choose **New region**.
2. Fill in:
   - **Name** and **Code** (capital letters, digits and underscores, such as `JP`; fixed once created).
   - **Currency** (ISO code, such as `JPY`; fixed once created because prices, purchases and reports are stored in it) and the **Currency symbol** position (before or after the amount; applies to every region in that currency).
   - **Coin value**: what one coin is worth in that currency, up to six decimals.
   - **Time zone**.
   - **Countries**: a country belongs to one region; picking one another region lists **moves** it here.
3. Leave **Active** off and choose **Save region**.
4. The new region starts as a **copy of the default region's** coin packs, VIP plans, title prices, ad limits and rewards, with money prices converted between the two coin values. Review them on each tab.
5. Turn **Active** on.

</Steps>

## Change, make default, delete

- **Turn a region off**: its countries use the default region for new sessions. Purchases, unlocks and price locks keep their region.
- **Make default**: this region then serves every country no active region lists. Viewers keep the prices they already locked. Exactly one region is the default, and it's always on.
- **Delete**: only a region that no viewer, purchase, unlock or report belongs to.

Every change can carry an optional reason and is recorded in the audit log.

## How regions work

- **Every country belongs to one region.** A country no active region lists uses the default region.
- **Prices are set per region.** A title without its own price in a region uses the default region's.
- **The coin value turns coins into money.** The app shows every price in coins **and** in the region's money before every unlock, and reports convert with it.

Exchange rates into your reporting currency are set separately, in <Path>Settings › Regions & languages</Path>. See [Regions & currencies](../../monetization/regions-and-currencies.mdx).
