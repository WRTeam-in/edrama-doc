## Payments

<Path>Settings › Payments</Path>

### Revenue share

Saved on each unlock and purchase, so a change never rewrites past money.

| Setting | Default | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **Default creator share** | 50% | A new creator's share of what their series earn. A creator's own percent, set on their page, wins. |
| **Google Play fee** | 15% | The store's cut, used for "after store fee" figures and creator pools. |
| **App Store fee** | 15% | |
| **Reason for the change** | | Recorded in the audit log. New unlocks and purchases use the new values; past ones keep theirs. |

### Creator price limits

What a creator may ask for an episode, and the free episodes every creator series starts with. Prices already set stay until they next change.

| Setting | Default |
|---|---|
| **Lowest coin price** | 30 coins |
| **Highest coin price** | 90 coins |
| **Free episodes at least** | 5 |

### Daily spend cap

The daily coin limits viewers can choose in the app ("spend guard"). The cap itself is a fair-play rule and stays on.

| Setting | Default |
|---|---|
| **Choices, in coins** | 100, 300, 600, 1000 (press Enter after each) |
| **Default cap** | 300 (must be one of the choices) |

You can switch the whole daily limit off in <Path>Settings › App & support › Features</Path>.

### Balance adjustments

**Approval limit** (default **1,000 coins**): above this, a second member must approve a coin adjustment ([Balance adjustments](../audience/balance-adjustments.mdx)).

### Store keys

Two cards let your server check purchases and hear about refunds and renewals:

- **Google Play**: package name, service account JSON, and the optional Pub/Sub push service account, plus the **real-time notifications push URL** to copy. See [Google Play](../../integrations/google-play.mdx).
- **App Store**: bundle ID, issuer ID, key ID, private key (.p8) and environment (Production or Sandbox), plus the **server notifications URL (version 2)** to copy. See [App Store](../../integrations/app-store.mdx).

## Creator revenue {#creator-revenue}

<Path>Settings › Creator revenue</Path> controls how each month's ad and VIP money is shared with creators ([Creator revenue](../money/creator-revenue.mdx)).

| Setting | Default | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **Prepare each month by itself** | On | On the import day, the month before is imported from AdMob (when connected) and calculated, and members with Transactions (Edit) get a bell. |
| **Import day of the month** | 5 | 1 to 28. AdMob finalises a month's earnings in the first days of the next. |
| **Ads pool** | 100% | Of each region's unlock-ad earnings. |
| **VIP pool** | 100% | Of each region's VIP revenue, after refunds and store fees. |
| **A second member approves** | Off | Whoever last changed a month (a pool or an adjustment) can't approve it. |

Each creator's own share percent is on their page and starts from the default share. A country's earnings go to the region that lists it.

### AdMob Reporting API

A card to connect AdMob **read-only**, so the monthly import can read your unlock ads' earnings by country and ad unit:

<Steps>

1. In Google Cloud, enable the **AdMob API**, set up and **publish** the OAuth consent screen (in testing mode, Google ends the connection after 7 days).
2. Create an **OAuth client ID** of type **Web application** and add the **Authorised redirect URI** shown on the card.
3. Paste the **OAuth client ID** and **client secret**, and **Save**.
4. Choose **Connect AdMob** and sign in with the Google account that owns the AdMob account. The **refresh token** (and the **publisher ID**, when there's one AdMob account) are filled in for you.

</Steps>

See [AdMob](../../integrations/admob.mdx).
