Payments and creator revenue settings

Payments​

Admin Panel›Settings›Payments

Revenue share​

Saved on each unlock and purchase, so a change never rewrites past money.

SettingDefaultMeaning
Default creator share50%A new creator's share of what their series earn. A creator's own percent, set on their page, wins.
Google Play fee15%The store's cut, used for "after store fee" figures and creator pools.
App Store fee15%
Reason for the changeRecorded in the audit log. New unlocks and purchases use the new values; past ones keep theirs.

Creator price limits​

What a creator may ask for an episode, and the free episodes every creator series starts with. Prices already set stay until they next change.

SettingDefault
Lowest coin price30 coins
Highest coin price90 coins
Free episodes at least5

Daily spend cap​

The daily coin limits viewers can choose in the app ("spend guard"). The cap itself is a fair-play rule and stays on.

SettingDefault
Choices, in coins100, 300, 600, 1000 (press Enter after each)
Default cap300 (must be one of the choices)

You can switch the whole daily limit off in Admin Panel›Settings›App & support›Features.

Balance adjustments​

Approval limit (default 1,000 coins): above this, a second member must approve a coin adjustment (Balance adjustments).

Store keys​

Two cards let your server check purchases and hear about refunds and renewals:

  • Google Play: package name, service account JSON, and the optional Pub/Sub push service account, plus the real-time notifications push URL to copy. See Google Play.
  • App Store: bundle ID, issuer ID, key ID, private key (.p8) and environment (Production or Sandbox), plus the server notifications URL (version 2) to copy. See App Store.

Creator revenue​

Admin Panel›Settings›Creator revenue controls how each month's ad and VIP money is shared with creators (Creator revenue).

SettingDefaultMeaning
Prepare each month by itselfOnOn the import day, the month before is imported from AdMob (when connected) and calculated, and members with Transactions (Edit) get a bell.
Import day of the month51 to 28. AdMob finalises a month's earnings in the first days of the next.
Ads pool100%Of each region's unlock-ad earnings.
VIP pool100%Of each region's VIP revenue, after refunds and store fees.
A second member approvesOffWhoever last changed a month (a pool or an adjustment) can't approve it.

Each creator's own share percent is on their page and starts from the default share. A country's earnings go to the region that lists it.

AdMob Reporting API​

A card to connect AdMob read-only, so the monthly import can read your unlock ads' earnings by country and ad unit:

  1. In Google Cloud, enable the AdMob API, set up and publish the OAuth consent screen (in testing mode, Google ends the connection after 7 days).
  2. Create an OAuth client ID of type Web application and add the Authorised redirect URI shown on the card.
  3. Paste the OAuth client ID and client secret, and Save.
  4. Choose Connect AdMob and sign in with the Google account that owns the AdMob account. The refresh token (and the publisher ID, when there's one AdMob account) are filled in for you.

See AdMob.
