<Path>Settings › Legal pages</Path> holds the pages shown in the app, on the website and at `https://your-domain.com/legal/{page}`. **Each edit publishes a new version; earlier ones are kept**, so what a viewer or creator agreed to can always be shown later.

| Page | Address | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| **Terms of service** | `/legal/terms` | The app's terms. Opened in the app from <Path panel="app">Me › Settings › Terms of use</Path>. |
| **Privacy policy** | `/legal/privacy` | Required by both stores. |
| **Creator terms** | `/legal/creator-terms` | Creators must accept the version in force before creating a series. |
| **Content licences & credits** | `/legal/licences` | Credits for licensed content and fonts. |
| **Refund policy** | `/legal/refunds` | Linked from the app's Help & refunds. |
| **Community guidelines** | `/legal/community` | The rules for comments and reviews. |
| **Takedown requests (DMCA)** | `/legal/takedown` | How rights holders report content. |

## Write and publish

<Steps>

1. Choose **Edit** (or **Write** for a page never published).
2. Enter the **Title** and the **Text** in Markdown: `## headings`, lists, `**bold**` and `[links](https://…)` show in the app.
3. Under **Languages**, optionally write the same page in each app language. Empty shows the default language's text. When you change the terms, change these too: they're published with the same version.
4. Choose **Publish**.

</Steps>

The list shows each page's **version** and when it was updated.

:::warning[Creator Terms]
Creators can't create a series until the **Creator terms** are published. When you publish a **new version**, every creator is asked to read and accept it again before continuing.
:::

## Search & sharing

Choose **Search & sharing** on a page to set, per language, the **search title** (about 60 characters), the **search description** (about 150 characters) and whether to **hide it from search engines**. Empty boxes keep the page's own title and first lines.
