Settings

Settings holds everything an operator configures without touching code. It's one page with a menu of sections down the side, in five groups, and a Save bar at the bottom.

Permission area: Settings
  • View See settings
  • Edit Change them
  • Owner Two-factor requirement, AI Studio keys and billing

The sections​

GroupSectionWhat it holds
Brand & lookBrandingApp name, tagline, logos, favicon, wordmark font, theme colour, the Studio's default theme
Sign-in screenThe photos, motion, poster strip and words of the sign-in pages
App & contentRegions & languagesApp languages and their files, the Studio language, the reporting currency and exchange rates
App & supportApp versions and store links, support email, feature switches, maintenance mode, comment blocklist
Video pipelineUpload size, YouTube episodes, creator upload limits, release delay
Storage & videoWhere files live (this server, Amazon S3, Cloudflare R2) and what streams videos (this server or Cloudflare Stream)
AI StudioSwitch, models, limits, prices, keys and billing
MoneyPaymentsRevenue share, store fees, creator price limits, spend cap choices, adjustment approval limit, Google Play and App Store keys
Creator revenueMonthly import, pool percents, approval, AdMob Reporting API
MessagesNotificationsPush limits and quiet hours, notification wording, Firebase keys, system alerts
Email delivery (SMTP)Your email server
Email templatesThe wording of every team and creator email
Trust & accessLegal pagesTerms, privacy, Creator Terms, refunds and more, versioned
Sign-in & securityRequire two-factor for the whole team

Integration keys​

Keys for outside services sit on cards at the end of their section. Every card has a Where do I find these? guide with the exact steps and links, the webhook or redirect address to copy, Save and Test connection.

CardSection
Google Play, App StorePayments
AdMob Reporting APICreator revenue
Amazon S3 bucket, Cloudflare R2 bucket, Cloudflare StreamStorage & video
Firebase, System alertsNotifications
SMTP serverEmail delivery (SMTP)
Secrets are write-only

Keys are stored encrypted and never shown again after saving: the field reads "Saved. Leave empty to keep it." Type a new value only to replace it. A value that came from the server's .env file says so; saving one here replaces it. Save before Test connection: it checks the keys as saved.
