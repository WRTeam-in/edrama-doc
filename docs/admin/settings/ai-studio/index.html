AI Studio settings

Admin Panel›Settings›AI Studio controls AI Studio for both the team and creators. Creators make characters, storyboards and video takes in the Creator Portal on their own Gemini key or the platform's; the team makes them for your own series. Whatever creators make still goes through review like an upload.

Keys and billing (Owner only)​

Who pays decides whose Google account is billed:

ModeMeaning
Creator managed (default)Every creator adds their own Gemini key and pays Google directly. Your limits don't apply to their key.
Admin managedEvery creator's calls go on the platform's key, within the limits and caps below. Creators don't add a key.
HybridEach creator may use the platform's key, their own, or both, and chooses. Limits apply only to calls on the platform's key.
SettingDefault
In Hybrid, creators may useBoth keys (the creator chooses)
In Hybrid, before a creator choosesThe creator's own key
Cap on the platform's key a month$100: every call on it, creators' and the team's. Blank for no cap.
Each creator's allowance a month$10 on the platform's key. Each creator's page can set otherwise.
Team studio a month$50. The team's AI Studio always uses the platform's key.

The platform's Gemini key is added on its own card: use a key from a Google project with billing on; Google checks it before it's saved. It's stored encrypted and only its last 4 characters are shown. Removing it stops new calls on it (creators who use it are told, never moved to their own key).

What creators may pick​

Limits per creator on the platform's key​

They apply only to calls the platform pays for. Days and months run in UTC. Each creator's page can override them.

SettingDefault
Images a day / a month100 / 1,500
Takes a day / a month40 / 600
Calls at once2 (keeps a Tier 1 Google key under its limit of 8 a minute)
Characters per series20
Prompt length2,000 characters
Keep unused files30 days (rejected storyboards, unkept takes and unused versions are deleted after this)
Ask to confirm images above$0.10 (every take always asks to confirm)

Prices for estimates​

What Google charges, used for the estimate on every button and in budgets. Check Google's pricing page and update these when it changes.

ItemDefault

A model without a price here takes the highest price listed for that size.

Per creator​

On Admin Panel›Community›Creators, a creator's AI Studio action shows their key status, who pays now and this month's usage, and lets you Allow AI Studio for them, set their limits on the platform's key, and (Owner only) which keys they may use in Hybrid and their monthly allowance.
