Branding and sign-in screen

Branding​

Admin Panel›Settings›Branding sets your identity once for the app, the Studio, the Creator Portal, the public pages and the website.

SettingDetails
App nameReplaces "eDrama" everywhere: the app (at its next start), the Studio, emails, share pages. Default eDrama.
TaglineDefault Stories that hook you. Prices that don't.
Horizontal logo for dark backgroundsWith a light name. Replaces the mark and name in the sidebar and on share and error pages.
Horizontal logo for light backgroundsWith a dark name. Top bar and public pages in the light theme.
Vertical logoThe mark above the name, with a light name, for the dark sign-in screens.
FaviconThe browser-tab icon. Square, at least 64 × 64.
Square logoThe mark alone, for small spots such as the push preview.
Wordmark fontInstrument Serif Italic or Geist Bold, when no logo is uploaded.
Theme colourGreenlight (default), Ember, Iris, Rosé, Aqua, Gold, Sky, or Custom colour (any hex code). Text on it switches between ink and white automatically so it stays readable. Charts keep their own palette.
Studio opens inPaper (light), Ink (dark) or Follow the device. Each member can still switch with the moon in the top bar.

Logos are PNG or WebP, up to 1 MB, on a transparent background. The mobile app's own logo ships inside the app (Logo & icons).

The previews show the theme colour on the app (a "Continue E7" card and an "Unlock E8 · 60 coins" button) and on the Studio. Choose Save: the Studio and Creator Portal change at once; the app picks it up at its next start.

Sign-in screen​

Admin Panel›Settings›Sign-in screen designs the stage beside the sign-in pages of the Studio and the Creator Portal.

SettingDetails
PhotosBuilt-in photos or Your own photos: up to 3 landscape photos, JPEG, PNG or WebP, up to 4 MB each (1600 × 1000 or larger looks best). Drag to set their order; fewer than 3 repeat.
MotionCinematic (photos cross-fade and drift, posters scroll, the tagline rises word by word), Subtle (only the photos cross-fade, slowly) or Still. Anyone whose device asks for less motion always gets a still stage.
Show the live titles' postersA strip of the newest live titles beside the photos. Off: the photos fill the stage.
WordsThe Tagline and Line under it, separately for Studio sign-in and Creator Portal sign-in. Leave one blank to keep the standard wording shown in grey.
tip

The built-in photos are stock images. Replace them with your own key art before launch.
