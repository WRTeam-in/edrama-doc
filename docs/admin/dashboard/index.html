Dashboard

The Dashboard answers one question: how is the platform doing? It opens when you sign in. Every member sees it, but each card shows only if your role can see that area: revenue cards need Transactions (View), viewer figures need Users (View).

Reading the numbers​

CardWhat it shows
RevenueMoney from coin packs and VIP in the range, in your reporting currency, with the change against the previous period of the same length, today's revenue so far, and the amount after store fees. Coin-pack revenue always equals the net on Transactions for the same days and region.
New viewersNew accounts in the range, split into signed in and guests.
Daily active viewersThe average number of viewers active each day.
Paying viewersViewers who paid in the range.
Revenue by sourceA daily stacked chart of coin packs and VIP. Switch between Before store fee and After store fee. The Table button shows the same data as a table.
How viewers unlockA donut of unlock methods. Useful for seeing whether ads, coins or VIP drive your catalogue.
Top titlesThe top 10 titles, ranked by Revenue or Views, with their status.
Needs attentionLive counts: review items waiting (and how long the oldest has waited), failed uploads, episodes held past their publish time, refunds and balance adjustments waiting for approval. Each row links to where you act on it.

How fresh are the figures?​

The Dashboard reads pre-counted figures, so it stays fast however big your catalogue gets. A background task recounts today and the two days before every 15 minutes, plus any day where a refund or a late purchase changed things. The line under the revenue card says when it was last counted.

Need today's numbers now? Choose Recount now. It recounts today straight away (at most once a minute for the whole team).

Regions and currencies​

Use the Region switcher in the top bar to see one region, or All regions for everything.

All money is shown in your reporting currency (USD by default), converted at the exchange rate in force on each day. If a currency has no exchange rate for some days, that revenue is left out and the card names the currency, rather than guessing. Add the missing rate in Admin Panel›Settings›Regions & languages.

note

Days are counted in UTC (universal time), so "today" may start a few hours earlier or later than your local midnight.
