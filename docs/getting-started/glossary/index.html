Glossary

The words you'll meet in eDrama and in these pages, in alphabetical order.

TermMeaning
Ad unlockWatching one rewarded ad (30 seconds at most) to unlock one episode.
AdMobGoogle's ad network. eDrama uses its rewarded ads, which the viewer chooses to watch in return for something.
Age ratingAll, 13+, 16+ or 18+, set on each title.
APIThe set of addresses the app uses to ask your server for data and actions, under /api/v1.
Append-onlyA record that is only ever added to, never edited or deleted. The coin ledger and the audit log are append-only.
Audit logThe history of every important change in the Admin Panel: who did it, when, and why.
Auto-unlockAn option a viewer can turn on: the next locked episode is unlocked with coins as it starts. Off by default and always held to the daily spend limit.
Bonus coinsFree coins from the welcome bonus, the daily spin, ads or a coin pack's bonus. They expire and are always spent first.
Category (also trope)A genre label such as "Revenge" or "Rebirth". Titles have categories; viewers pick favourites during onboarding. In the code they are called tropes.
CoinThe app's currency. Bought in coin packs, spent to unlock episodes. A coin's money value is set per region.
Coin valueWhat one coin is worth in a region's currency, for example 1 coin = 0.0099 USD.
CreatorAn invited person or studio who publishes their own series through the Creator Portal and earns a share of the revenue.
Creator shareThe percent of a series' revenue that goes to its creator. Default 50%.
CronA server feature that runs a command on a timetable. eDrama needs one cron line that runs every minute.
DropThe release of a new episode. "Drop alerts" tell followers about it.
Exchange rateHow much one unit of a currency is worth in your reporting currency, from a date.
ffmpegA free program that converts video. eDrama uses it to turn uploads into streams.
FirebaseGoogle's app platform. eDrama uses it for viewer sign-in and push notifications.
Free episodesThe first episodes of a series anyone can watch without paying. The first locked one is the paywall.
GuestA viewer using the app without signing in. Guests can watch and earn bonus coins but can't buy.
HLSThe streaming format eDrama uses: the video is cut into small pieces at several qualities, and the player picks the best one for the connection.
Home builderThe Admin Panel screen where you design the app's Home tab from sections.
IdempotentSafe to repeat. If a viewer taps "Unlock" twice or the connection retries, they are charged once.
LedgerThe list of every coin in and out of a viewer's wallet, with the balance after each line.
Paid coinsCoins bought with money. They never expire.
Price lockOnce a viewer unlocks their first episode of a series with coins, their per-episode price for that series can never rise. A lower price still applies.
Push notificationA message that appears on the phone's lock screen, sent through Firebase.
Queue / queue workerA waiting list of background jobs and the process that works through it.
RegionA group of countries that share a currency, prices, coin packs, VIP plans, ad limits and rewards.
Reporting currencyThe one currency the Dashboard and totals are shown in. Default USD.
Rewarded adAn ad the viewer chooses to watch, in return for an unlock or bonus coins.
Series PassOne price in coins that unlocks every episode of a series, including future ones.
SMTPThe standard way servers send email. You give eDrama your email provider's SMTP details.
Spend guard (daily spend limit)The most coins a viewer allows themselves to spend in a day. Unlocks past it pause and ask first.
SSV (server-side verification)AdMob telling your server directly that an ad was watched to the end, so an ad can't be faked.
StreakThe number of days in a row a viewer has used the daily spin.
StudioAnother name for the Admin Panel.
TitleA series in the catalogue. Every title has an owner type: eDrama Original (made by you), Licensed (from a studio) or Creator.
Two-factor (2FA)Signing in with a code from an authenticator app as well as the password.
VIPA weekly, monthly or yearly subscription that unlocks every episode with no ads.
Welcome bonusBonus coins a new account gets once per device. Default 100 coins for 7 days.
