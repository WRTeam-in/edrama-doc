Use this page as your map. Each line links to the page with the full instructions. Tick them off in order: later steps depend on earlier ones. **Your ticks are saved in this browser**, so you can come back to it as you work.

<Checklist id="launch">

<ChecklistGroup title="1. Server">

<ChecklistItem id="server-rent">Rent a server that meets the [requirements](../installation/overview.mdx) (PHP 8.5, MySQL 8, HTTPS, ffmpeg or Cloudflare Stream) and point your domain at it.</ChecklistItem>

<ChecklistItem id="server-prepare">Prepare it: [VPS](../installation/vps-server.mdx) or [shared hosting](../installation/shared-hosting.mdx). The domain points at the package's `public` folder only.</ChecklistItem>

<ChecklistItem id="server-install">Run the [web installer](../installation/web-installer.mdx): requirements, purchase code, database, Owner account.</ChecklistItem>

<ChecklistItem id="server-cron">Add the [cron line and queue workers](../installation/cron-and-queues.mdx), then check <Path>System › System</Path> shows "Cron running".</ChecklistItem>

<ChecklistItem id="server-video">Make sure [ffmpeg works](../installation/video-processing.mdx) (or choose Cloudflare Stream) and a test episode reaches **Ready**.</ChecklistItem>

</ChecklistGroup>

<ChecklistGroup title="2. Admin Panel basics">

<ChecklistItem id="admin-2fa">Sign in as the Owner and [turn on two-factor](../admin/sign-in-and-account.mdx).</ChecklistItem>

<ChecklistItem id="admin-branding">Set your [branding](../admin/settings/branding.mdx): app name, logos, favicon, theme colour.</ChecklistItem>

<ChecklistItem id="admin-smtp">Add your [SMTP email server](../admin/settings/email.mdx) and send a test email.</ChecklistItem>

<ChecklistItem id="admin-firebase">Add the [Firebase keys](../admin/settings/notifications.mdx) and press **Test connection**.</ChecklistItem>

<ChecklistItem id="admin-support">Set your [support email](../admin/settings/app-and-support.mdx). Until you do, the app shows `support@example.com`.</ChecklistItem>

<ChecklistItem id="admin-storage">Choose [where files and videos live](../admin/settings/storage-and-video.mdx).</ChecklistItem>

<ChecklistItem id="admin-legal">Publish your [legal pages](../admin/settings/legal-pages.mdx), including the Creator Terms.</ChecklistItem>

<ChecklistItem id="admin-languages">Upload the app file for every [language](../admin/settings/regions-and-languages.mdx) you offer, or turn Hindi off (a fresh install lists it as offered).</ChecklistItem>

<ChecklistItem id="admin-team">Invite your [team](../admin/system/team-and-roles.mdx) with the right roles, and require two-factor for everyone.</ChecklistItem>

</ChecklistGroup>

<ChecklistGroup title="3. Money">

<ChecklistItem id="money-regions">Review your [regions](../admin/money/regions.mdx): currency, coin value and countries.</ChecklistItem>

<ChecklistItem id="money-rates">Add [exchange rates](../admin/settings/regions-and-languages.mdx) for every currency you sell in.</ChecklistItem>

<ChecklistItem id="money-packs">Set your [coin packs and VIP plans](../admin/money/coin-packs-and-vip.mdx) per region.</ChecklistItem>

<ChecklistItem id="money-stores">Create the same products in [Google Play](../integrations/google-play.mdx) and the [App Store](../integrations/app-store.mdx), paste the store keys in <Path>Settings › Payments</Path>, and set up both stores' server notifications.</ChecklistItem>

<ChecklistItem id="money-admob">If you use ads, set up [AdMob](../integrations/admob.mdx) with server-side verification on every ad unit.</ChecklistItem>

<ChecklistItem id="money-share">Set the [creator share and store fees](../admin/settings/payments-and-revenue.mdx).</ChecklistItem>

</ChecklistGroup>

<ChecklistGroup title="4. Content">

<ChecklistItem id="content-categories">Review the [categories](../admin/content/categories.mdx).</ChecklistItem>

<ChecklistItem id="content-titles">Create your first [titles](../admin/content/titles.mdx), upload episodes and set prices.</ChecklistItem>

<ChecklistItem id="content-schedule">[Schedule](../admin/content/release-schedule.mdx) the episodes.</ChecklistItem>

<ChecklistItem id="content-home">Build the app's [Home](../admin/content/home-builder.mdx) and publish it.</ChecklistItem>

<ChecklistItem id="content-demo">Remove the sample content with [Clear demo data](../admin/system/clear-demo-data.mdx) if you imported it.</ChecklistItem>

</ChecklistGroup>

<ChecklistGroup title="5. Mobile app">

<ChecklistItem id="app-computer">[Set up Flutter](../app-setup/flutter-setup.mdx) and the Android and iOS tools, and read [what's in the download](../app-setup/overview.mdx).</ChecklistItem>

<ChecklistItem id="app-package">Change the [package name](../app-setup/name-and-package.mdx) first: Firebase and the stores are tied to it.</ChecklistItem>

<ChecklistItem id="app-firebase">Connect [Firebase](../app-setup/firebase.mdx) (the build stops without its files), fill in the iOS URL schemes and turn on the sign-in methods you offer.</ChecklistItem>

<ChecklistItem id="app-server">[Connect the app](../app-setup/connect-to-server.mdx) to your server, then [run it](../app-setup/run-the-app.mdx).</ChecklistItem>

<ChecklistItem id="app-brand">Change the [name](../app-setup/name-and-package.mdx), [logo and icons](../app-setup/logo-and-icons.mdx) and [colours](../app-setup/colors-and-fonts.mdx).</ChecklistItem>

<ChecklistItem id="app-push">Upload your APNs key to Firebase and send yourself a [test push](../app-setup/push-notifications.mdx).</ChecklistItem>

<ChecklistItem id="app-ads">Replace Google's test IDs with your [AdMob app IDs](../app-setup/ads-setup.mdx).</ChecklistItem>

<ChecklistItem id="app-links">Set up [share links](../app-setup/deep-links.mdx), and check both `.well-known` files on your server show your app.</ChecklistItem>

<ChecklistItem id="app-signing">Create and back up your [upload keystore](../app-setup/signing.mdx), and add every SHA fingerprint to Firebase.</ChecklistItem>

<ChecklistItem id="app-release">[Build the release](../app-setup/release-build.mdx), create a [store reviewer account](../installation/commands.mdx#store-reviewer-account) and [publish it to the stores](../app-setup/publishing.mdx).</ChecklistItem>

<ChecklistItem id="app-versions">Enter the store links and versions in <Path>Settings › App & support</Path>.</ChecklistItem>

<ChecklistItem id="app-check">Once the store build works, turn on [App Check](../integrations/firebase.mdx#app-check): `monitor` first, then `enforce`.</ChecklistItem>

</ChecklistGroup>

<ChecklistGroup title="6. Website">

<ChecklistItem id="web-deploy">[Deploy the website](../website/deploy.mdx) to Vercel or your own Node.js server.</ChecklistItem>

<ChecklistItem id="web-connect">[Connect it](../website/connect.mdx): set the **Website address** and the refresh secret, then publish a change and see it appear within a minute.</ChecklistItem>

</ChecklistGroup>

<ChecklistGroup title="7. Before you announce it">

<ChecklistItem id="final-purchase">Buy a coin pack with a test account and check it on [Transactions](../admin/money/transactions.mdx).</ChecklistItem>

<ChecklistItem id="final-push">Send a [test push](../admin/audience/push.mdx) to your own phone.</ChecklistItem>

<ChecklistItem id="final-share">Share a series from the app and tap the link on another phone: the app opens on that series.</ChecklistItem>

<ChecklistItem id="final-jobs">Check <Path>System › System</Path> shows no failed jobs.</ChecklistItem>

</ChecklistGroup>

</Checklist>

:::tip
Steps 1 and 5 can run in parallel if two people are working: the app only needs the server's address, which you know before the server is finished.
:::
