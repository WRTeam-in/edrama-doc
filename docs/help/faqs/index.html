Frequently asked questions

Each answer has its own link: hover a question and choose Share to copy it.

Installation and server​

What are the default login details?

There are none. You choose the Owner email and password in the installer's last step. If you lost them, run php artisan edrama:create-owner on the server only if no Owner exists; otherwise use Forgot password? on the sign-in page (email must be set up).

Do I need Composer, Node.js or a build step on the server?

No. The package includes vendor/ and the built public/build. Upload, point the domain at public, and run the web installer.

Can I install eDrama on shared hosting?

Yes, if you can choose PHP 8.5 and allow proc_open. Video processing needs ffmpeg in your home folder, or use Cloudflare Stream so your server does no video work. One cron line runs both the schedule and the queues. See Shared hosting.

Episodes don't release at their time, and pushes don't go out. Why?

The scheduler isn't running. Add the cron line from Admin Panel›System›System›Server setup and wait until it shows Cron running. See Cron & queues.

Can I jump straight to the newest version?

No. Updates go one version at a time: install 1.0.0 → 1.1.0, then 1.1.0 → 1.2.0. Back up first. See License & updates.

Can I move to a new domain?

The purchase code is registered to one domain. After moving, the panels ask for the code again at /activate. If it's refused as registered elsewhere, contact WRTeam support with your purchase code to release it. Step by step: Move to another domain. Your viewers' app keeps working meanwhile: the license never locks the API.

/install shows "404".

The installer closes itself once the install finishes: sign in at /admin instead. It also answers 404 while EDRAMA_INSTALLER isn't true in .env. To install again on an empty database, contact support.

The installer marks something "Missing".

Your server lacks a PHP extension, runs PHP below 8.5, or a folder isn't writable. Fix the items marked Missing (or ask your host), then choose Check again. See Server installation.

An episode stays "Processing".
  • With this server or Amazon S3: ffmpeg isn't found, or the host stops long jobs. Check the ffmpeg line on Admin Panel›Settings›Storage & video, and that the cron line runs (Video processing).
  • With Cloudflare Stream: the webhook isn't registered. Run php artisan edrama:video-webhook (Cloudflare Stream).

Videos stuck in processing are also re-checked every 10 minutes.

Emails don't arrive.

Until you add an SMTP server in Admin Panel›Settings›Email delivery (SMTP), emails are only written to the server's log and nobody receives them. Emails also go out through the queue, so the cron line must be running (Email).

Content​

Why won't an episode publish? It says it's under the floor.

Paid episodes must be at least 45 seconds long. Upload a longer cut, or open the title's Episodes tab and use Allow short episode with a reason. Creators can't submit episodes under the floor.

I changed Home but the app still shows the old one.

Changes in the Home builder reach the app only after Publish layout. Viewers see it the next time they open Home.

A live title doesn't appear for some viewers.

Check its Where it's shown (title editor › Monetization): it may be limited to some regions. Also check it has a price in the default region, and that the viewer's audience matches the Home section it's in.

Money​

A viewer paid but says the coins didn't arrive.

Search their order ID on Admin Panel›Money›Transactions and open the purchase: the four verification steps show where it stopped. Purchases waiting on the store finish by themselves; the viewer can also choose Restore purchases in the app. If needed, use a balance adjustment with a reason.

Why can't I refund an App Store purchase from the panel?

Apple decides App Store refunds. Send the viewer to Apple's refund request page (linked in the purchase drawer); Apple's notification updates the purchase when it's done.

I raised a series' price but some viewers still pay the old one.

That's the price lock: viewers who already started the series keep their price. A rise doesn't reach them; a drop does. See Coins & pricing.

The Dashboard says some revenue is left out.

A currency has no exchange rate into your reporting currency for some days. Add the rate in Admin Panel›Settings›Regions & languages.

Can I offer a free trial for VIP?

No. It's a locked fair-play rule: VIP never starts with a free trial that turns into a charge. The server refuses a subscription that started with one.

Viewers watch ads but nothing unlocks.

Server-side verification isn't set up. In AdMob, set the callback URL shown in Admin Panel›Money›Monetization›Ads on every rewarded ad unit. See AdMob.

A viewer moved country and sees the wrong prices.

If they've never paid, the app moves them to the right region by itself at its next start. If they have paid, open their page in Admin Panel›Audience›Users and use Change region, with a reason (Regions).

Can I show a series in some countries only, or charge different prices per country?

Yes, both. On the series' Monetization tab, set Where it's shown to Only these regions. For different prices, create a region for those countries in Admin Panel›Money›Monetization›Regions, with its own currency, coin value, packs, VIP plans and title prices (Regions & currencies).

How are creators paid?

eDrama works out each creator's share every month and writes a statement. Your team pays them outside eDrama (bank transfer, UPI, PayPal, Wise or other) and records the payment with Record payout on the creator's page, so nobody is paid twice. Creators see their statements and payouts on Earnings in the Creator Portal (Creator earnings).

Creators​

A creator didn't get their invite email.

Email delivery may not be set up: add your SMTP server in Admin Panel›Settings›Email delivery (SMTP), send a test, then use Resend invite on the creator. Invites expire after 7 days.

A creator can't sign in to the Creator Portal.

Check that the Firebase Web API key and Auth domain are in Admin Panel›Settings›Notifications, and that your domain is in Firebase's Authorized domains. Creators sign in with Google, email or phone; Sign in with Apple isn't available in the portal, and a guest account can't become a creator.

Creators can't create a series.

The Creator terms must be published in Admin Panel›Settings›Legal pages, and each creator must accept the version in force.

The app​

I changed the app name in Settings, but the name under the icon didn't change.

The name under the icon is part of the app build: change it in AndroidManifest.xml and Info.plist and publish a new version. See Name & package name.

Shared links open in the browser, not the app.

The share domain must match in app_config.dart, build.gradle.kts and Runner.entitlements, and your server must publish the link files (EDRAMA_ANDROID_PACKAGE, EDRAMA_ANDROID_SHA256, EDRAMA_IOS_APP_ID). See Deep links.

"Continue with Apple" fails.

Enable the Apple provider in Firebase Authentication with your Apple Developer account, and check Sign in with Apple is on in Xcode's capabilities.

How do I add a language without releasing the app?

Add it in Admin Panel›Settings›Regions & languages, upload its translated JSON file for the mobile app, and turn on Offered in the app. The app downloads it at its next start. See Languages.

The app can't reach my server.

Check apiBaseUrl in lib/app/config/app_config.dart: it must be your server, use https:// and end in /api/v1/. If you turned on EDRAMA_APP_CHECK=enforce, go back to monitor until your store build passes App Check (Connect to your server).

The app shows "maintenance" or asks to update.

Maintenance mode is on, or the app's version is below Minimum supported. Both are in Admin Panel›Settings›App & support (App & support).

What login do I give Google Play and App Store reviewers?

Create an email-and-password account for them on the server with php artisan edrama:review-account review@your-domain.com, and put it in each store's review notes (Server commands).

Didn't find your answer?

Check Troubleshooting for step-by-step fixes, or contact WRTeam support with your purchase code.
