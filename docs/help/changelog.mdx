## Version 1.0.0

The first release of eDrama.

### Viewer app (Flutter, Android & iOS)

- **[ADDED]** Vertical player with swipe between episodes, double-tap like, hold for 2×, speed, quality (Auto to 1080p for VIP), subtitles, Up Next and clean view. [Player](../viewer-app/player.mdx)
- **[ADDED]** Guests with a welcome bonus; sign-in with Google, Apple, phone and email, with guest history carried over. [Accounts](../viewer-app/accounts.mdx)
- **[ADDED]** Unlock with coins, one rewarded ad, Series Pass or VIP, with auto-unlock and the daily spend guard. [Unlocking](../viewer-app/unlocking-episodes.mdx)
- **[ADDED]** Rewards: daily spin, streaks, shields, titles and ads for coins. [Rewards](../viewer-app/rewards.mdx)
- **[ADDED]** Coin store, append-only wallet history, VIP with renewal reminders. [Coins & wallet](../viewer-app/coins-and-wallet.mdx)
- **[ADDED]** Home built from the server, search, browse by category, creator pages, Library, inbox, comments and reviews.
- **[ADDED]** Languages from the server (right-to-left supported), light and dark themes, offline cache, screen protection.

### Admin Panel

- **[ADDED]** Dashboard, Titles and title editor, Categories, Upload & pipeline (resumable, CSV, URL and YouTube), Release schedule, Home builder and Website. [Admin Panel](../admin/overview.mdx)
- **[ADDED]** Creators, Review queue, Comments & reviews, AI usage.
- **[ADDED]** Users, Balance adjustments (with second approver), Support tickets, Push.
- **[ADDED]** Monetization per region (regions, coin packs, VIP, ads, rewards, fair-play rules), Transactions with refunds and exports, Creator revenue.
- **[ADDED]** Settings, Team & roles with 18 permission areas, Audit log, System, License & updates, Clear demo data.
- **[ADDED]** Storage on this server, Amazon S3 or Cloudflare R2; video on this server (ffmpeg) or Cloudflare Stream.
- **[ADDED]** AI Studio with Gemini: characters, storyboards, takes and the episode editor.

### Creator Portal

- **[ADDED]** Apply or invite, Creator Terms, series, uploads, review submissions, release times, stats, comments and reviews, earnings, AI Studio, notices. [Creator Portal](../creator/overview.mdx)

### Installation

- **[ADDED]** Four-step web installer with demo data, license activation and one-version-at-a-time updates. [Installation](../installation/overview.mdx)
