The website and the Admin Panel need to know about each other:

- the **website** needs your server's address, to read its content;
- the **Admin Panel** needs the website's address, to show previews and to tell it about changes.

The two share a **refresh secret**, so only your Admin Panel can ask the website to refresh.

## The website's settings

They go in `.env.local` on your computer, or in your host's environment variables (on Vercel: **Settings › Environment Variables**).

| Variable | Needed | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| `EDRAMA_API_URL` | **Yes** | Your server's address **without** `/api`, such as `https://admin.your-domain.com`. Must be `https://` (plain `http://` only works for `localhost`, `127.0.0.1` and `*.test`). |
| `REVALIDATE_SECRET` | Recommended | The refresh secret from the Admin Panel (below). Without it, a publish shows within 5 minutes instead of at once. At least 32 characters, which the Admin Panel's secret always is. |
| `NEXT_PUBLIC_SITE_URL` | No | The website's own address, used only while the Admin Panel has none set. |
| `EDRAMA_IMAGE_HOSTS` | No | Extra hosts images may come from, comma-separated, when your files live on S3 or a CDN, such as `my-bucket.s3.amazonaws.com,cdn.your-domain.com`. Your server's host is always allowed. |
| `EDRAMA_SCRIPT_HOSTS` | No | Extra `https://` origins allowed to serve analytics scripts, for self-hosted Plausible or Umami. Plausible, Umami Cloud and Google Analytics are allowed already. |
| `EDRAMA_FRAME_ORIGINS` | No | Other addresses your Admin Panel opens at, comma-separated, that may show the website's live preview. Your server's own address always may. |
| `APP_ADS_TXT` | No | The lines of `/app-ads.txt`, which AdMob checks on the developer website listed in your store pages. Copy them from AdMob › **Apps › app-ads.txt**, one per line (or separated by `\n`). Without it, `/app-ads.txt` isn't served. |

:::warning[Rebuild after changing them]
`EDRAMA_API_URL`, `EDRAMA_IMAGE_HOSTS`, `EDRAMA_SCRIPT_HOSTS` and `EDRAMA_FRAME_ORIGINS` are built into the site's security headers and image settings. After changing any of them, **build and deploy the website again**.
:::

:::tip[Files on Amazon S3?]
If you moved files to S3 or another S3-compatible bucket in <Path>Settings › Storage & video</Path>, add the bucket's host (or its **Public URL** host) to `EDRAMA_IMAGE_HOSTS`, or posters won't show on the website.
:::

## In the Admin Panel

<Steps>

1. **Open the Connection tab.** Go to <Path>Content › Website</Path> and open **Connection & history**. It shows **This server's address for the website (EDRAMA_API_URL)**: that's the value for `EDRAMA_API_URL`.
2. **Set the Website address.** Enter where visitors open the website, such as `https://www.your-domain.com`, and choose **Save address**. It's used for previews, canonical links, the sitemap, share pictures, and to tell the website about changes.
3. **Make the refresh secret.** Choose **Make a secret** (once one exists, the button reads **Make a new secret**, and the old one stops working). The secret is **shown once**: copy it into the website's `REVALIDATE_SECRET`, then redeploy (or restart) the website so it reads the new value.

</Steps>

<Access area="Website" view="Open the page" edit="Change the content" />

Changing the **Website address** or making a secret needs a role with **Settings › Edit** ([Roles & access](../getting-started/roles-and-access.mdx)).

## Check it worked

1. Change a heading on the **Sections** tab and choose **Publish**.
2. Open the website: the new heading shows **within a minute**.

If it only shows after about 5 minutes:

- the secret doesn't match: make a new one, paste it again and redeploy;
- the server's queue isn't running: the refresh is sent as a background job ([Cron & queues](../installation/cron-and-queues.mdx));
- or the website runs on `localhost` or a private network address, which never receives the call.

## How the refresh works

Each time you publish, or switch the website on or off, your server sends `POST <Website address>/api/revalidate` with a small JSON body, signed with the refresh secret (an HMAC-SHA256 in the `X-Edrama-Signature` header). The website refuses calls that aren't signed with its `REVALIDATE_SECRET` or are more than 5 minutes old. The call is only sent to a **public** address, never to `localhost` or a private network, and if it fails nothing breaks: pages rebuild by themselves every 5 minutes.
