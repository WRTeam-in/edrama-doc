## The pages

| Address | Page |
|---|---|
| `/` | Home, built from your sections in <Path>Content › Website</Path> |
| `/series` | All series, with category chips |
| `/series/<slug>` | One series: banner, poster, synopsis, creator, an **Open in app** button and the store buttons |
| `/categories` and `/categories/<slug>` | All categories, and the series in one |
| `/creators/<slug>` | A creator's profile and series |
| `/sitemap.xml`, `/robots.txt` | For search engines |
| `/llms.txt`, `/llms-full.txt` | For AI assistants, only when you allow AI crawlers and `llms.txt` (<Path>Content › Website</Path> › Search & statistics) |
| `/app-ads.txt` | For AdMob, only when `APP_ADS_TXT` is set ([Connect](connect.mdx#the-websites-settings)) |
| `/s/<id>`, `/c/<handle>` | The app's own share links: they redirect to the series or creator page |

The website shows what viewers in your **default region** see. A series limited to other regions isn't on it, so include the default region if a series should appear on the website.

## Languages

- The **default language** is at plain addresses: `/series/…`, `/categories/…`.
- Every other language turned on in <Path>Content › Website</Path> › **Look & languages** lives under its code: `/hi/series/…`, `/ar/series/…`. Only languages already offered in the app (<Path>Settings › Regions & languages</Path>) can be picked.
- Right-to-left languages such as Arabic are laid out right to left.
- Visitors switch language from the header when **Show a language menu in the header** is on, and light or dark when **Let visitors switch between light and dark** is on.
- Website texts are translated on the **Translations** tab. Series and category names use the translations already entered for the app.
- The sitemap lists every page in every language, with links between the language versions.

## Links that open the app

The website serves the two files phones read to open links in your app instead of the browser:

- `/.well-known/assetlinks.json` (Android)
- `/.well-known/apple-app-site-association` (iOS)

Both are filled from your server's app settings, so they stay empty until the server knows your app: set `EDRAMA_ANDROID_PACKAGE`, `EDRAMA_ANDROID_SHA256` and `EDRAMA_IOS_APP_ID` in the server's `.env`. Your app must list the website's domain too, as `websiteHost` on Android and an `applinks:` entry on iOS ([Deep links](../app-setup/deep-links.mdx)).

## What comes from elsewhere

| On the website | Set in |
|---|---|
| App name, logos, theme colour | <Path>Settings › Branding</Path> |
| Google Play and App Store buttons | <Path>Settings › App & support</Path> › App versions › **Store link** |
| Series, categories, creators | <Path>Content › Titles</Path>, <Path>Content › Categories</Path>, <Path>Community › Creators</Path> |
| Legal pages in the footer | <Path>Settings › Legal pages</Path> |
| Sections, header, footer, theme, translations, search and analytics | <Path>Content › Website</Path> ([Website](../admin/content/website.mdx)) |
