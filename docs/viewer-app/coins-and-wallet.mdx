## Buying coins

The coin packs appear on the **Rewards** tab (under **Top up**) and in the coin store an unlock opens.

| On a pack | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **1,000 coins · +200 bonus** | The coins and the bonus on top. |
| **Popular** / **Best value** | Flags your team sets. |
| **≈ 16 episodes** | About how many episodes it unlocks at the region's prices. |
| **First top-up: double coins** | A one-time offer: *300 + 300 bonus for $1.99 · once per account*. |

Under the packs: *Prices in INR · India*, *Paid coins never expire. Bonus coins expire after 7 days and are spent first.* and *Prices include tax. You confirm with Google Play next.*

<Steps>

1. Tap a pack. A guest is asked to sign in first.
2. Confirm in Google Play or the App Store.
3. The server checks the receipt, then: *+1,200 coins added · 1,000 paid · 200 bonus*, with the new balance. Coins fly to the balance.

</Steps>

If the app closes mid-purchase, the purchase is picked up at the next start. **Restore purchases** (<Path panel="app">Me › Restore purchases</Path> or the wallet) checks the store for anything already bought: *All set: 3 purchases checked, nothing missing.*

The history is **append-only**: *nothing is edited or deleted. Refunds and corrections appear as new rows with a reason you can see.* Tap a row for its details: the change, paid and bonus coins, balance after, the episode, and a reference to copy when contacting support.

## Spend guard

**Spend guard** is a daily limit on coins, *like screen time, for spending*. Pick **Coins a day** from the choices your team sets (the default is marked). Unlocks past the limit pause and ask first; auto-unlock never goes past it. If you switch the daily limit off in the Admin Panel, it disappears from the app.
