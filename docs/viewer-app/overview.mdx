The viewer app is what your audience downloads from Google Play and the App Store. It's built for **vertical episodes of 60 to 90 seconds**: open a series, swipe up for the next episode, and unlock more when the free ones run out. It runs on Android 7+ and iOS 15+, on phones and tablets, in light and dark themes, and in every language you offer (including right-to-left ones).

## The four tabs

| Tab | What's there |
|---|---|
| **Home** | The sections your team built: featured series, Continue watching, rows, banners, categories. See [Home & discovery](home-and-discovery.mdx). |
| **Library** | **Watching** (series in progress), **My List** (saved for later) and **Unlocked** (episodes and Series Passes you own). See [Library & notifications](library-and-notifications.mdx). |
| **Rewards** | The daily spin, the streak, titles, ads for coins, the coin store and the wallet. When Rewards is switched off, this tab becomes **Wallet**. See [Rewards](rewards.mdx). |
| **Me** | The profile, VIP, settings, help and account. See [Settings & help](settings-and-help.mdx). |

Swipe between tabs or tap them. Tapping the tab you're on scrolls it back to the top. Pressing back on any tab other than Home goes Home.

## Offline and slow connections

- What's already loaded keeps working offline; a banner says *You're offline. What's loaded still plays.*
- Every list the app loads is saved on the phone and shown when the server can't be reached.
- **Data saver** (in onboarding and Settings) starts every episode at 360p, then sharpens, and loads the next episode later.

## What the app learns from your server

At each start, the app reads its configuration from your server: the app name and theme colour, which features are on, the viewer's region with its prices, and the languages. So most changes you make in the Admin Panel reach viewers **without a new app release**. If your server is in [maintenance mode](../admin/settings/app-and-support.mdx#maintenance-mode), viewers see a "Back soon" screen instead of the catalogue.
