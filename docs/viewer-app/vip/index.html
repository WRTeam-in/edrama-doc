VIP

VIP is a subscription: every drama unlocked, no ads, ever. Open it from App›Me›VIP, the unlock sheet, or a VIP banner.

Subscribe​

  1. Read What's included (the perks your team wrote) and The terms, plainly.
  2. Choose a plan: Weekly, Monthly or Yearly. Renews every month until you cancel. First renewal: 24 Dec.
  3. Tap Start Monthly · $19.99, check the summary (Renews automatically on 24 Dec until you cancel. We remind you 24 hours before each renewal.) and Subscribe in the store.
  4. You're VIP. Every series unlocked, no ads. If the store is still confirming: Almost there. VIP turns on as soon as it does.

A guest is asked to sign in first.

While VIP​

  • Every locked episode plays without coins or ads, and ads never show anywhere.
  • 1080p quality is available in the player.
  • The daily spin pays double (by default).
  • The VIP page says You're VIP. Monthly plan · renews on 24 Dec for $19.99. We remind you on 23 Dec.

Renewal reminders​

About 24 hours before every charge, the viewer gets a notification and an inbox message. These reminders can't be switched off, so a renewal never surprises anyone.

Cancel​

Cancel VIP explains that cancelling happens in the store and opens it: If you do, VIP stays on until 24 Dec, nothing more is charged. After cancelling, the page shows VIP ends on 24 Dec. Nothing more is charged. with Turn renewal back on.

If VIP is switched off by your team, the page says VIP is not available right now; viewers who already paid keep their VIP.
