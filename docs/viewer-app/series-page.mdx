## What it costs

The cost card shows the full price **before** the viewer spends anything, in coins and in their own money:

| Part | Example |
|---|---|
| **Free** | *E1–E5*, no ads to start |
| **Catch up** / **Finish** | The coins to unlock the released episodes they haven't unlocked yet |
| **Series Pass** | *≈ $1.49 · all 24*, when the series has one |
| **The rule** | *60 coins (≈ $0.59) an episode, never more. Or 1 ad ≤ 30 s per episode, watched to the end.* |
| **Price locked** | Shown once they've started: their price can't rise. |

The card changes for VIP members (*Included with VIP · No ads · Every released episode is unlocked for you*), for viewers with a Series Pass (*Series Pass active · Paid in full*), and when every released episode is free.

## The episode grid

Each tile shows the episode's state: **Free**, **Unlocked**, **Locked · 60 coins**, **Watched**, **Now** (where they are), or **Not yet released**. Tapping a locked one opens the [unlock sheet](unlocking-episodes.mdx).

## Further down

- **About**: the synopsis (**More** / **Less**) and **Created by**, with the creator's followers.
- **Ratings & reviews**: the score, reviews, and **Rate** once they've reached episode 3 ([Comments & reviews](comments-and-reviews.mdx)).
- **More like this**: similar series.

A series limited to some regions isn't shown to viewers elsewhere; a link to it says it isn't available in their region.
