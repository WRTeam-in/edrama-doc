The series page

Tapping a series opens its page. The poster flies into place, so it feels instant even while the rest loads.

What it costs​

The cost card shows the full price before the viewer spends anything, in coins and in their own money:

PartExample
FreeE1–E5, no ads to start
Catch up / FinishThe coins to unlock the released episodes they haven't unlocked yet
Series Pass≈ $1.49 · all 24, when the series has one
The rule60 coins (≈ $0.59) an episode, never more. Or 1 ad ≤ 30 s per episode, watched to the end.
Price lockedShown once they've started: their price can't rise.

The card changes for VIP members (Included with VIP · No ads · Every released episode is unlocked for you), for viewers with a Series Pass (Series Pass active · Paid in full), and when every released episode is free.

The episode grid​

Each tile shows the episode's state: Free, Unlocked, Locked · 60 coins, Watched, Now (where they are), or Not yet released. Tapping a locked one opens the unlock sheet.

Further down​

  • About: the synopsis (More / Less) and Created by, with the creator's followers.
  • Ratings & reviews: the score, reviews, and Rate once they've reached episode 3 (Comments & reviews).
  • More like this: similar series.

A series limited to some regions isn't shown to viewers elsewhere; a link to it says it isn't available in their region.
