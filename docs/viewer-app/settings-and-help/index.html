Me, settings and help

The Me tab​

The Me tab shows the viewer's profile (or Sign in for guests), VIP is on for members, and three stats: Coins, Day streak and Titles. Below:

RowOpens
My ListEverything they saved.
WalletTheir balance and every coin in and out.
VIPThe VIP page.
Spend guard300 coins a day · 180 used today.
Restore purchasesGets back anything already bought.
PricesFrom the country you're in: their region and currency.
Invite friendsShares the app: I'm watching short dramas on eDrama. Try it: …
Become a creatorYour creator application form (while applications are open).
