## Splash

The app opens on the splash screen: your logo draws itself, with the tagline *Stories that hook you. Prices that don't.* and, at the bottom, "Made by" your company. Meanwhile the app signs the viewer in as a **guest** (no account needed) and reads its configuration from your server.

## Onboarding (first launch only)

Two short steps, then Home. No account is needed.

<Steps>

1. **What hooks you?** The viewer picks **three or more** categories. They feed Home's "Because you like…" rows.
2. **Watch in your language.** They pick the **app language** from the languages you offer, and can turn on **Data saver** (start every episode at 360p, then sharpen; good on slow networks).
3. **Start watching.** Home opens. *You can change all of this in Settings.*

</Steps>

## Screens your server can show instead

| Screen | When |
|---|---|
| **Back soon** | [Maintenance mode](../admin/settings/app-and-support.mdx#maintenance-mode) is on. Shows your message. *Your coins, unlocks and progress are safe.* |
| **Update required** | The app's version is below the **minimum supported** version. The viewer must update; an **Update now** button opens the store. |
| **A new version is ready** | A newer version than theirs is marked **latest**. They can update or carry on. |

Set the versions in <Path>Settings › App & support › App versions</Path>.
